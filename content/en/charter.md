# SERVER 504 CHARTER — V2

## Preamble
Server 504 is a community of autonomous alliances that choose to cooperate for long-term stability, competitive strength, fair access to shared benefits, and success in cross-server content.

This Charter establishes the permanent principles of cooperation. It is not intended to control the internal management of any alliance. Operational rules are defined in the Server 504 Operational Codex, while season-specific arrangements are defined in the Seasonal Strategic Annex.

---

## Article 1 — Server First, Without Erasing Alliance Autonomy
1. Signatory alliances agree that major inter-alliance decisions should consider the long-term strength, stability, competitiveness, and player retention of Server 504.
2. Each alliance remains fully autonomous in its internal affairs, including membership, ranks, internal event rules, leadership appointments, and internal resource management.
3. The 504 Council has authority only over matters that affect multiple alliances, shared server assets, cross-server operations, or obligations expressly accepted under this Charter and the Operational Codex.
4. “Server interest” may not be used by itself to override an explicit rule. Any temporary exception must follow the Emergency Coordination procedure.

## Article 2 — Equality of Alliances in Governance
1. All signatory alliances have equal dignity regardless of power, ranking, age, or spending level.
2. Greater combat strength may justify greater operational responsibility, but it does not create additional political votes.
3. Unless otherwise stated, governance follows the principle: **one eligible alliance, one vote**.

## Article 3 — Mutual Respect and Good Faith
Signatory alliances and their members shall act with respect, good faith, reasonable transparency, and a preference for de-escalation. Personal rivalries, grudges, and internal alliance politics should not be converted into server-wide conflicts.

## Article 4 — Internal Non-Aggression
1. Signatory alliances shall maintain a server-wide non-aggression framework.
2. Intentional attacks, rallies, truck looting, gathering interference, territory disruption, or other deliberate harm against protected Server 504 players are prohibited except where expressly authorized under the Operational Codex.
3. Accidental first-time incidents resolved promptly through apology and reasonable restitution should normally not be treated as disciplinary offenses.

## Article 5 — Capital Rotation
1. Capital control shall rotate among eligible main alliances according to a schedule agreed at the beginning of each season.
2. The rotation list is locked for that season unless an eligible alliance dissolves, merges, voluntarily withdraws, or becomes unable to perform the role.
3. Presidency is a server service responsibility, not a political supremacy.
4. The presiding alliance receives no additional Council vote and no casting vote merely because it controls the Capital.

## Article 6 — Shared Strategic Assets
1. Capital benefits, Armory access, Mining Centers, strategic territories, and other shared assets shall be managed through published rules or schedules wherever practical.
2. No alliance has permanent ownership of a shared strategic asset.
3. Temporary priority may be granted for a defined server objective, but the reason and duration must be stated and normal allocation restored afterward.

## Article 7 — Collective Defense
1. Cross-server defense is a shared responsibility.
2. Alliances may assume different operational roles depending on the event, including Combat Core, Rally Support, Defense, Development, Point Optimization, Logistics, or Reserve.
3. These roles are operational assignments only and do not create permanent classes of “war alliances” and “development alliances.”

## Article 8 — The 504 Council
1. The 504 Council is a coordination body between autonomous alliances, not a government above them.
2. Each eligible alliance appoints one voting representative and may appoint one deputy.
3. A deputy may vote only when replacing the primary representative; no alliance may cast more than one vote.
4. Working groups may include additional experts, but working-group membership does not create additional political votes.

## Article 9 — Voting Thresholds
Unless a more specific rule applies:
- Routine operational decisions: simple majority of eligible votes cast.
- Territory restructuring, major shared-benefit changes, sanctions, and major diplomacy: two-thirds majority.
- Migration policy, Charter amendment, internal military authorization, or permanent governance reform: three-quarters majority.
- A tied vote does not create authority to change the status quo.

## Article 10 — Conflict of Interest and Recusal
1. A Council representative directly involved in a dispute, investigation, sanction, or material personal conflict must recuse from the relevant investigation and vote.
2. The alliance directly accused in a formal disciplinary case does not vote on the sanction concerning that case.
3. Recusal does not remove the right of the affected party to present evidence or defend itself.

## Article 11 — Evidence, Due Process, and Proportionality
1. Formal sanctions require evidence proportionate to the seriousness of the allegation.
2. Parties must be given a reasonable opportunity to respond.
3. Sanctions shall target the responsible player or alliance as narrowly as practical.
4. Collective punishment is prohibited unless the alliance itself formally adopts, directs, or repeatedly protects the offending conduct.
5. Mediation, correction, restitution, or de-escalation should be preferred when they can realistically restore stability.

## Article 12 — Transparency of Decisions
1. Council discussions may remain confidential when necessary.
2. Material Council decisions shall be summarized publicly to signatory leadership with, at minimum: decision, reason, voting threshold/result, duration if temporary, and review date when applicable.
3. Strategic war plans, migration negotiations, sensitive rosters, and active diplomatic negotiations may remain confidential until disclosure is safe.

## Article 13 — Emergency Coordination
1. When immediate action is necessary to protect Server 504 during a major military, diplomatic, migration, or operational crisis, the Council may activate Emergency Coordination by a two-thirds vote.
2. Emergency measures must be limited to the stated crisis and expire automatically after 72 hours unless renewed by another two-thirds vote.
3. Emergency status does not suspend the principles of alliance autonomy, recusal, or proportionality beyond what is strictly necessary for the crisis.

## Article 14 — Migration and Player Retention
1. Migration is a strategic server resource and shall be coordinated through a season-specific policy.
2. Recruitment should consider server-wide manpower gaps, activity coverage, rally capability, alliance health, and newcomer integration.
3. No alliance should make migration promises that materially conflict with an agreed server-wide seat or placement policy.
4. Newcomers should receive a clear placement and integration plan rather than being treated as transferable assets.

## Article 15 — Alliance Health and Voluntary Mergers
1. Alliance health shall be evaluated using multiple indicators such as active membership, event participation, leadership capacity, online coverage, rally capability, recruitment outlook, and ability to maintain assigned responsibilities.
2. Total Alliance Power may be considered but shall not be the sole trigger for intervention.
3. The Council may facilitate or recommend a merger but may not compel one.

## Article 16 — Seasonal Review
1. The Charter should be reviewed at the beginning of each new season but amended only when a permanent principle truly needs to change.
2. Operational details should normally be changed in the Codex or Seasonal Strategic Annex instead of rewriting the Charter.
3. Charter amendments require a three-quarters majority of eligible signatory alliances.

## Article 17 — Withdrawal and Accession
1. Alliances may join by accepting this Charter, the Operational Codex, and the current Seasonal Strategic Annex.
2. An alliance may withdraw by formally notifying the Council.
3. Withdrawal removes future treaty privileges and protections but does not erase obligations arising from incidents that occurred before withdrawal.

## Final Principle
**Alliances manage Server 504 together. The Council exists to coordinate their agreement, not to replace their leadership.**
