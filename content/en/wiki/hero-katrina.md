# Katrina

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 15 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Rider normal-attack offense  
> **Naming note:** community sources use **Katrina**; official patch history has also used **Katerina**.

Katrina is a Rider combat hero focused on **normal-attack damage output**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Katrina from:

- the Lucky Chest event;
- Elite Recruitment Tickets in the Radio Station.

Availability can vary with event rotation and State progression.

## Skill structure

### Reaper's Missiles — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero pattern.

### Rapid Fire — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Ironclad Rush — Passive Skill

Public data describes Katrina as increasing Riders' attack damage, with star progression increasing **troop normal attack damage**.

This gives her a more specific offensive identity than generic ATK/HP heroes: she is strongest when the formation and surrounding systems reward repeated normal attacks.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Rider offensive specialist / formation-dependent.**

Katrina is most valuable when:

- Rider is the main faction;
- normal-attack damage is an important share of the march's output;
- her star level is already competitive with newer alternatives;
- the rest of the formation does not require a more defensive Rider slot.

Her value should be judged from combat reports rather than CP alone: compare normal-attack contribution before and after swapping her out.
