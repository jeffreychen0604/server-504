# Megan

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Shooter  
> **Specialty:** Construction

Megan is a Shooter hero whose defining contribution is **construction progression**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Megan as recruitable through **Prime Recruitment Tickets** in the Radio Station.

## Skill structure

### Divine Prayer — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill.

### Holy Orb — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Genius Architect — Progression Skill

Public data lists:

- additional free construction time;
- reduced basic construction resource cost;
- increasing construction speed at higher star breakpoints;
- larger free-construction windows through progression.

This makes Megan especially relevant during shelter growth and Industrial-era planning even if she is not a preferred late-game Shooter combat slot.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Progression utility / keep invested while construction matters.**

Best-fit use cases:

- Watchtower prerequisite chains;
- Industrial upgrades;
- construction scoring windows;
- reducing long-cycle build time and resource burden.
