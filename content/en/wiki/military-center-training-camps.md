# Military Center & Training Camps

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Strong community reference for roles; exact tables are version-sensitive

The shelter military layer separates **army capacity** from **faction-specific troop training**.

## Military Center

Community references describe the Military Center as increasing:

- **Unit Cap** — the size of the account's troop pool;
- **Troop Capacity** — the amount a formation can carry/use;
- account CP.

This makes the Military Center strategically important even when it is not the immediate Watchtower prerequisite.

## Fighter / Rider / Shooter Camps

Each faction has its own training camp. Camp progression is tied to the troop tier and training capacity available for that faction.

Because troop-tier unlock tables conflict across public sources, this article does not duplicate a fixed T1–T10 chart. Use [Troop Tier Progression](#/wiki/troop-tier-progression) for the conflict audit.

## Military Center versus Training Ground

Do not confuse them:

- **Military Center:** army/formation capacity system.
- **Faction Camps:** train the actual faction units.
- **Training Ground:** throughput/speed support whose exact current effect still needs verification.

## Main-faction planning

If resources are constrained, keeping the primary faction camp near the progression ceiling usually creates more immediate combat value than leveling every camp equally. However, hard Watchtower prerequisites can still force secondary-camp upgrades.
