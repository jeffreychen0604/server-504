# Sealed Island — Season Buildings

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** Medium-high for building roles; exact upgrade costs remain community-derived and should be verified in-game.

Sealed Island progression is gated less by normal shelter CP than by its temporary seasonal systems. The two most important personal progression structures are the **Oni Seal Hall** and **Quartz Factory**.

## Oni Seal Hall

The Oni Seal Hall is the core resistance building for Sealed Island. Community guides consistently identify it as the primary way to raise **Virus Resistance**, which determines how efficiently troops can fight infected seasonal enemies and participate in higher-resistance seasonal objectives.

Practical priority:

1. keep the Oni Seal Hall moving before chasing optional seasonal upgrades;
2. compare your current Virus Resistance with the requirement shown on the target before attacking;
3. avoid spending Quartz in ways that delay the next resistance breakpoint.

The exact resistance gained per level and upgrade costs can change between season versions, so the Server 504 Wiki should not freeze an old cost table without screenshots from the current season.

## Quartz Factory

Quartz Factories produce **Quartz**, a major upgrade resource for advanced seasonal structures, especially the Oni Seal Hall.

Community guides describe a seasonal resource loop in which:

- **Stone** is used to establish or develop Quartz-producing structures;
- **Quartz** is generated over time and then spent on higher-tier seasonal progression;
- additional Quartz sources can appear in missions, passes, shops or event rewards.

Official patch notes later mentioned that Sealed Island received **more Mints**, confirming that the season's economy has been adjusted after launch. That is another reason resource tables should be versioned.
