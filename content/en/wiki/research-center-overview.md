# Research Center — Overview

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Official system identity + community structure data

The **Research Center** is the shelter building used for technology progression. Research is one of Dark War: Survival's main permanent account-growth systems because it improves economic efficiency, development speed, troop performance and event-specific scoring.

## What the Research Center does

Public current-era sources consistently show several research families:

- **Development** — account infrastructure, queues, capacity and efficiency.
- **Economy** — gathering and resource production.
- **Battle** — troop/faction combat improvements.
- **Duel Research** — Alliance Duel scoring and reward efficiency.
- **Battle Strategy** — late competitive combat progression that consumes Wisdom Medals.

The exact tree layout, prerequisites and costs are version-sensitive. The Wiki therefore treats tree names and confirmed effects as stable reference, while exact node cost tables remain subject to Server 504 verification.

## Building progression

Community structure data places the Research Center unlock around **Watchtower Lv.8** and shows the building increasing Research Speed as it levels. A Fandom structure table lists a progression from roughly **+0.1% at Lv.1 to +3.0% at Lv.30**, but this is community-maintained data and should not be treated as a current official cost/effect table without in-game confirmation.

The Research Center also appears repeatedly as a Watchtower prerequisite at higher progression stages, which makes it both a technology building and a shelter-progression gate.

## Why research planning matters

Research consumes several distinct resources depending on the tree:

- ordinary resources and Research Speedups;
- **Wisdom Medals** for advanced Duel / Battle Strategy progression;
- time, which can often be more limiting than resources;
- event timing, because Research CP, Research Speedups and Wisdom Medal consumption can all become scoring actions.

This is why a good account does not simply research the shortest available node. It chooses a node whose permanent value and event timing justify the cost.

## Current high-confidence patch note

Official 2026 patch history explicitly references:

**Research Center → Battle Strategy → Kill Training**

and states that Kill Training technologies take effect on **War Days (Wednesday & Saturday)** during the Origin Lands season. This confirms Battle Strategy is an actively maintained live research branch, not only legacy community terminology.

## Data quality rule

The Wiki will publish exact research costs only when one of these conditions is met:

1. current Server 504 screenshots show the node and cost;
2. current official patch notes state the value; or
3. multiple current sources agree and the value is clearly version-labeled.

Old full-tree screenshots are useful for discovery, but not enough to overwrite current in-game UI.
