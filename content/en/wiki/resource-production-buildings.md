# Resource Production Buildings

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Strong community reference; exact production tables are version-sensitive

The shelter has four core passive production buildings:

| Building | Primary resource |
| --- | --- |
| Lumber Mill | Wood |
| Scrapyard | Iron |
| Power Plant | Electricity |
| Hunter's Hut | Food |

Wood, Iron and Electricity are the main construction/research/training resource family. Food is also important for hero and shelter progression, but public guides differ on exactly which systems consume it at each stage.

## Hunter's Hut

Community structure data describes the Hunter's Hut as producing Food and increasing production/storage with upgrades.

## Lumber Mill, Scrapyard and Power Plant

These buildings supply the three core base resources. Their strategic value changes over time:

- early game: passive production helps keep construction moving;
- mid game: long upgrades and troop/research spending increase demand sharply;
- late game: resource stockpiles may stop being the primary bottleneck when Precision Parts or other rare materials become the limiting factor.

## Production versus gathering

Shelter production is only one source. Radar tasks, map gathering, events, item packs and successful raids can also provide resources. The correct planning model is therefore:

`passive production + map gathering + event rewards + protected inventory − planned spending`

## Do not copy old production tables blindly

Public building tables may contain old base values or omit bonuses from heroes, research, Alliance Tech, VIP and temporary effects. The Wiki therefore records **function first** and keeps exact production-per-hour tables pending Server 504 capture.
