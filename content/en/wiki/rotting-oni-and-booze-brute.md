# Rotting Oni & Booze Brute

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for official enemy names and balance changes; medium for community descriptions of solo/rally roles and drops.

**Rotting Oni** and **Booze Brute** are the main recurring infected enemy types associated with Sealed Island's Miasma and Blood Moon systems.

## Rotting Oni

Community guides consistently describe Rotting Oni as the more regular world-map seasonal hunting target. Its practical role is similar to a solo-progression enemy: clear levels, obtain seasonal rewards and move up as Virus Resistance improves.

## Booze Brute

Booze Brute is described by community guides as the larger, rally-oriented seasonal target. Alliance participation therefore matters more than isolated player power.

## Official balance history

The November 2025 official update confirms that these targets were actively tuned:

- recommended CP for Lv.1–65 Rotting Onis was lowered;
- recommended CP for Lv.1–65 Booze Brutes was lowered;
- Viral Resistance requirements for Lv.65+ **Blood Moon Rotting Onis** were reduced;
- Viral Resistance requirements for Lv.65+ **Blood Moon Booze Brutes** were reduced.

This is strong evidence that old CP/resistance charts can become obsolete even during the same season.

## Normal vs Blood Moon versions

The game distinguishes ordinary seasonal targets from **Blood Moon** variants. The Blood Moon versions should be documented separately when Server 504 captures their current:

- CP recommendation;
- Virus Resistance requirement;
- stamina/AP cost;
- rally requirement;
- reward pool;
- Magatama or other seasonal currency drops.

## Practical hunting rule

Do not judge these monsters only by the recommended CP number. For Sealed Island, a player with enough raw CP but insufficient Virus Resistance can still perform inefficiently.

A useful order of checks is:

1. Virus Resistance;
2. current formation strength;
3. target level/version;
4. whether it is a normal or Blood Moon target;
5. whether the target is intended for solo or rally play.
