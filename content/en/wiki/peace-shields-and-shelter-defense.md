# Peace Shields & Shelter Defense

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for current map/UI behavior; exact shield durations and item inventory remain version-sensitive.

Peace Shields are the primary shelter-protection tool on the world map. They should be understood together with City Gate defense, shelter durability and event-specific attack rules.

## Current confirmed behavior

Official patch history confirms that Peace Shield status remains visible on shelters even after zooming the world map. This matters for target selection during busy war periods.

The Alliance Shop currently sells Advanced Teleporters and historically has also been a common source of Peace Shields. Exact shield stock, duration and purchase limits should be checked against the live Server 504 shop rather than copied from an older guide.

## Defense is more than a shield

A shield prevents hostile interaction while valid, but an unshielded shelter still has several defensive layers:

- City Gate defense squads;
- hero and troop formation quality;
- reinforcements where allowed;
- Hospital capacity;
- Warehouse-protected resources;
- shelter durability.

Community building data describes the Warehouse as protecting a portion of Wood, Iron, Power and Coin from plunder. That is economic protection, not battle immunity.

## Event exceptions

Do not assume ordinary shelter rules remain unchanged inside special war zones. Trade Post / Merchant Guild dark zones, battlefield maps, Capital Lost Lands and seasonal areas can modify mortality, durability damage or attack restrictions.

Official updates have also tested event-day buffs that reduce losses from attacking shelters. Such buffs are explicitly version/event dependent and should never be treated as permanent PvP rules.

## Safe operational rule

Before dropping a shield, check four separate things:

1. whether your shelter can legally be attacked in the current event;
2. City Gate Defense Presets;
3. Hospital capacity;
4. whether your map position has special-zone modifiers.
