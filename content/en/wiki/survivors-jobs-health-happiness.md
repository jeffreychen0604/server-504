# Survivors, Jobs, Health & Happiness

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Official for the core loop; community for individual building interactions

The official game listing describes **Assign Jobs** as a core feature: survivors can be assigned to specialized work to improve shelter growth, while their **health and happiness** affect productivity.

## Core survivor loop

`Population capacity → assign survivors → produce/support → maintain health and happiness → preserve productivity`

This is a different layer from hero progression. Heroes drive combat, economy passives and special systems; ordinary survivors are part of the shelter workforce.

## Population

Dorms increase the number of survivors the shelter can house. More population is useful only when support systems keep pace: additional survivors still need food, rest and treatment.

## Jobs

Community guides show survivors being used in shelter production and support buildings. Exact worker-slot counts and per-worker output modifiers should be verified from the current UI before being added as a numeric table.

## Health

Sick survivors are associated with the shelter medical layer. Public sources disagree on the exact wording/function of **Infirmary** versus **Hospital**:

- community/Japanese references describe the **Infirmary** as treating sick survivors;
- other community pages blur Infirmary into wounded-unit healing;
- the **Hospital** is clearly tied to wounded combat units and capacity.

The Wiki therefore keeps survivor sickness and troop wounds separate until Server 504 captures resolve the terminology.

## Happiness / mood

The official listing confirms happiness affects survivor productivity. Community references associate the **Deerhunter Bar** with raising survivor mood. Exact happiness thresholds, penalties and restoration rates remain `VERIFY SERVER 504`.

## Practical rule

Do not treat survivor wellbeing as decorative early-game UI. If productivity falls when health/happiness falls, then support buildings are part of shelter throughput, just like construction and research speed.
