# Battle Strategy & Kill Training

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Official branch existence + current patch behavior; detailed node tree pending UI capture

**Battle Strategy** is an advanced Research Center branch associated with Wisdom Medal progression and competitive combat.

It should not be confused with the ordinary Battle research tree.

## Official current confirmation

Dark War: Survival's 2026 official patch history explicitly names:

**Research Center → Battle Strategy → Kill Training**

and states that **Kill Training technologies take effect on War Days — Wednesday and Saturday — during the Origin Lands season**.

This is important for two reasons:

1. Battle Strategy remains an actively maintained live system.
2. Some Battle Strategy effects can depend on event/season context rather than being permanently active everywhere.

## What Battle Strategy is for

Current community references describe Battle Strategy as a Wisdom Medal-gated combat progression path that becomes especially important after the account has established its Duel Research economy.

Exact branches may include troop combat, kill-event or war-day effects, but the Wiki will not reconstruct the full tree from screenshots or memory without current evidence.

## Kill Training

The official patch gives one current behavior rule:

- during **Origin Lands**, Kill Training technologies apply on **War Days (Wednesday & Saturday)**.

Do not extrapolate that statement into a universal all-season rule. Outside Origin Lands, or after future season changes, the active window may differ.

## Duel Research vs Battle Strategy

A useful decision framework:

**Duel Research** improves recurring reward/point efficiency.  
**Battle Strategy** spends the same scarce Wisdom Medal pool toward combat-oriented progression.

This creates an opportunity-cost problem. Community strategy usually recommends establishing the important Duel efficiency nodes first because they improve future Wisdom Medal income, then investing more aggressively into Battle Strategy.

However, an account preparing for a major competitive war may rationally prioritize a high-impact Battle Strategy breakpoint sooner.
