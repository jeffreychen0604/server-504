# Lucas

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Combat

Lucas is a Rider / Combat hero whose public kit combines **Rider damage** with reduced **counterattack damage taken** by the troop he leads.

## Acquisition

Community data lists:

- Legend Battle Pass;
- Elite Recruitment Tickets;
- selected special offers.

## Skill structure

### Sundering Strike — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill.

### Sword of Fate — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Indomitable Will — Passive Skill

Public data describes:

- increased Rider combat damage;
- progressively stronger reduction to counterattack damage taken by the troop led as stars increase.

That combination gives Lucas a mixed offensive/defensive identity rather than pure burst damage.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Main-Rider combat candidate.**

Lucas is best evaluated against the player's existing Rider investment. A new Lucas at low stars can be less efficient than a heavily developed older Rider even if later-generation community meta lists place him highly.

Before replacing an established Rider, compare:

- star gap;
- Universal Fragment cost;
- existing equipment;
- Exclusive Equipment investment;
- whether the account's APC/chips and research are already Rider-focused.
