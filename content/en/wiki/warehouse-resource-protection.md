# Warehouse & Resource Protection

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Strong community reference; exact protection table remains version-sensitive

The Warehouse protects part of the shelter's resources from being plundered during successful enemy attacks.

## What it protects

Community references consistently describe the Warehouse as increasing protected resource capacity. Older tables specifically track protection for the main resources and Coin, but exact values should not be assumed permanent because building tables can be affected by patches and account bonuses.

## Protected versus exposed resources

A useful planning model is:

`total stored resources − protected amount = potential exposed stock`

This matters when preparing large construction/research upgrades. A player holding a large unprotected stockpile is more attractive to raiders than a player whose resources are still inside consumable inventory items or below the protection threshold.

## Strategic use

- Upgrade the Warehouse enough that routine savings are not unnecessarily exposed.
- Before PvP windows, compare stored resources with current protection rather than relying on total RSS alone.
- Do not confuse Warehouse protection with Peace Shield protection: the Warehouse mitigates plunder after an attack; a shield prevents eligible shelter attacks while active.

## Version-sensitive data

Historical community tables list protection values through Lv.30, but this Wiki does not promote those tables to current canonical data until Server 504 screenshots verify the current effect values.

## Verify Server 504

- Resource types protected in the current tooltip.
- Protection amount at several representative levels.
- Whether Coin protection still exists as a distinct value.
- Interaction with VIP/research/other protection bonuses.
