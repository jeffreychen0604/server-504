# Adventure Camp & Exploration

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Adventure Camp, Exploration stages and idle/adventure rewards  
> **Confidence:** COMMUNITY — exact caps and output require current Server 504 verification

The **Adventure Camp** is a fixed utility facility tied to Exploration/adventure progression rather than a normal Lv.1–30 throughput building.

## Community-backed role

Current community references describe Adventure Camp as:

- a fixed facility used for Exploration and special adventure modes;
- not directly upgradeable like normal shelter buildings;
- connected to managing Exploration squads and stage progression.

A recent Season 6 community guide also describes Adventure progression as a significant **Food** source whose output scales with stage progress and accumulates as idle rewards.

## Idle reward discipline

Community guides warn that Adventure rewards can reach a storage/cap limit if left unclaimed too long. One recent guide cites an approximately **8-hour cap**, but this value has not been confirmed by an official current source and therefore remains provisional.

The safe operating rule is simple:

- collect before the in-game meter reaches cap;
- use the current UI timer/cap rather than relying on an old guide;
- continue pushing Adventure stages when the next stage materially improves idle income and your hero team can clear it efficiently.

## Guy interaction

Community hero data identifies **Guy** as an Adventure/economy specialist with progression effects tied to Food/Energy. Exact current values should be checked against the live hero skill tooltip because his type labeling has also conflicted across public sources.

## Exploration vs Radar

Do not merge these systems conceptually:

- **Adventure/Exploration** → stage progression and adventure/idle reward loop;
- **Radar** → Radar event tasks and world-map utility;
- **Gathering Ground** → Exploration attempts/rare-task support plus Apocalypse Guide access.
