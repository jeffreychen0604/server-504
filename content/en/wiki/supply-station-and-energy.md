# Supply Station & Energy

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Energy recovery/claim flow and world-activity preparation  
> **Confidence:** HIGH for current direct-claim behavior; COMMUNITY for refresh schedule/amounts

The **Supply Station** is the shelter utility associated with recovering **Energy** used for world exploration and related activities.

## Current official behavior

A recent official patch improved the low-Energy flow:

- when an activity prompts you to obtain more Energy, it can now be **claimed directly**;
- you no longer have to manually navigate back to the Supply Station first.

This is a quality-of-life change, not evidence that the underlying Energy refresh schedule or quantities changed.

## Community-backed refresh behavior

The Fandom Supply Station page currently states that Energy replenishment refreshes at:

- **00:00 server time**;
- **12:00 server time**;

with each window lasting 12 hours.

Because this exact schedule is not currently corroborated by an official public source, the Wiki treats it as community-backed until Server 504 UI confirms it.

## Guy interaction

Community data also associates **Guy** with additional daily Energy and Energy-recovery improvements through his progression skill. Exact current values should be read from the live hero tooltip.

## Practical use

Before a world-activity block:

1. check current Energy;
2. claim any available Supply Station recovery;
3. identify whether an event rewards Energy-consuming actions;
4. avoid capping free Energy recovery unnecessarily;
5. preserve consumable Energy items when a higher-value event is imminent.
