# Season Shop & VIP Shop

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Medium-high for recurring item categories; exact stock, currency cost and limits are version/season/VIP dependent.

Season Shop and VIP Shop are recurring sources for scarce progression materials, but neither should be documented with a permanent price table unless Server 504 provides a current screenshot.

## Season Shop

Historical and recent community references associate the Season Shop with materials such as:

- Power Cores;
- Boost Ores;
- Orange Versatile Hero Fragments;
- Orange equipment / equipment boxes;
- Titanium Alloy;
- Design Blueprints;
- season-specific permanent skins or account-wide bonuses.

Recent 2026 strategy guides increasingly recommend comparing temporary materials against **permanent season rewards** such as building/shelter skins or other account-wide bonuses before spending season currency.

### Season Shop rule

The best purchase is not always the rarest-looking item.

Ask:

1. Is the item a current hard bottleneck?
2. Can it be farmed elsewhere every week?
3. Is there a permanent season-limited reward that disappears when the season ends?
4. Does buying now unlock a meaningful breakpoint before the shop closes?

Exact inventory changes between seasons and server progression stages.

## VIP Shop

Legacy item databases list the VIP Shop as a source for:

- Power Cores;
- Boost Ores;
- Orange Versatile Hero Fragments.

Recent 2026 community guides also reference **Golden Wrenches** and Coins/Gold as useful VIP Shop purchases depending on account stage and event timing.

The shop's actual stock and value depend on VIP level and current patch, so the Wiki does not publish a universal VIP-level price table without in-game evidence.

## Timing with events

Materials that score in recurring events should usually be **purchased when available but consumed when useful**.

Examples:

- Golden Wrenches: hold for vehicle/APC-related scoring windows when possible;
- Power Cores: use when the main formation is ready and/or when the event schedule rewards the spend;
- Hero fragments: align star-up spending with relevant scoring windows when practical.

Do not delay an upgrade that materially affects an immediate server war simply for marginal event points.

## Paid packs are a separate economy

The Pack Shop / Special Offers are not the same thing as free exchange shops. Official 2026 patch notes continue to add/remove pack tabs and change purchase limits, including a Mod Vehicle Chip Pack and increased Precision Parts Pack limit.

Because paid inventory changes quickly, this Wiki records only stable system relationships, not pack-value rankings.
