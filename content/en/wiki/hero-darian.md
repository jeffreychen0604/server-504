# Darian

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 15 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Shooter  
> **Specialty:** Combat

Darian is a Shooter / Combat hero with an offense-oriented public kit centered on **Shooter ATK and troop skill damage**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists:

- Legend Battle Pass;
- Lucky Chest.

## Skill structure

### Absolute Order — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill with an artillery-support presentation.

### Leader's Pride — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Supreme Glory — Passive Skill

Public data describes:

- increased Shooter ATK;
- additional troop skill damage through star progression.

This makes Darian especially relevant to Shooter formations whose damage profile already benefits from skill-damage investment.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Shooter combat core candidate / keep invested when already developed.**

Darian should be compared to other Shooter combat heroes by actual account state rather than tier labels alone. Stars, standard equipment, troop research, APC chips and Exclusive Equipment all materially affect whether replacing him is efficient.
