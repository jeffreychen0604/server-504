# Hero Investment Framework

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Purpose:** Separate stable hero data from fast-changing meta recommendations.

A permanent tier list is a poor fit for Dark War: Survival because hero value changes with:

- State age and hero generation;
- faction research depth;
- star level and fragment availability;
- standard equipment quality;
- Exclusive Equipment availability and breakthrough;
- APC/chip alignment;
- the player's existing investment;
- PvP, boss, Adventure or progression goals.

The Server 504 Wiki therefore uses an **investment framework** instead of treating one public tier list as universal truth.

## 1. Decide whether the hero is Combat or Progression-first

### Combat-first examples

Examples in the current S-rarity dataset include:

- Tristan, Francis, Noah;
- Cyrus, Marcia, Katrina, Lucas;
- Natasha, Margaret, Darian, Rosa.

These heroes should primarily be evaluated against the player's main faction, formation role, stars, equipment and Exclusive Equipment.

### Progression-first examples

- **Quinn** — training and Alliance Duel value;
- **Corleone** — research value;
- **Megan** — construction value;
- **Evans** — Modified Vehicle progression;
- **Catherine & Rex** — Radar progression;
- **Guy** — Adventure food / energy value.

A progression hero can remain worth starring even if they are no longer a top PvP slot.

## 2. Main faction comes before isolated hero popularity

A strong hero from the wrong faction may add less account value than a slightly weaker hero who multiplies the player's existing:

- troop research;
- faction passives;
- troop tier investment;
- APC chips;
- equipment plan;
- formation presets.

For most accounts, deep investment into one primary faction produces better results than repeatedly switching because a new hero appears on a tier list.

## 3. Compare replacement cost, not just theoretical strength

Before replacing an established hero, compare:

- current star difference;
- fragment availability;
- skill investment;
- equipment already assigned;
- Exclusive Equipment investment;
- whether the new hero requires changing faction or formation structure.

A newer hero can be stronger at equal development while still being a poor immediate investment for a mature account.

## 4. Exclusive Equipment can change the answer

Official patch notes confirm that Exclusive Equipment is available to Orange-quality heroes once the State reaches the required progression age and the hero reaches 2 Stars. Exclusive Equipment adds combat attributes and a special combat skill.

Later systems added Red Star breakthrough, Hall of Honor progression and star-level swapping between Red Star Exclusive Equipment.

Therefore every future meta assessment should have two separate fields:

- **Without Exclusive Equipment**
- **With current Exclusive Equipment state**

Do not rank a hero as if these two states were equivalent.

## 5. Hero Battle role is separate from faction

Community testing describes Hero Battles / Adventure as having role behavior beyond faction labels. Tanker-type heroes provide frontline survival while Striker-type heroes provide sustained damage between skill rounds.

This means a formation can fail even when the faction is correct if the frontline/backline function is poor.

For individual hero pages, the Wiki should eventually record the current in-game role icon separately from faction and specialty.

## 6. Use a decision label instead of a permanent tier

Recommended labels for Server 504:

- **Core now** — currently important to the player's main formation;
- **Build if switching** — strong candidate when moving into that faction;
- **Progression utility** — worth building for economy/event value;
- **Keep invested** — older hero still efficient because existing stars/equipment are high;
- **Replace only after breakpoint** — new hero becomes efficient only after reaching a defined star/equipment threshold;
- **Verification pending** — public sources disagree or current Server 504 data is missing.

These labels are more actionable than `S+ / S / A` without context.

## 7. Update cadence

Stable fields such as name, faction and specialty should change rarely. Meta fields should carry their own date:

- `identity_verified_at`
- `skills_verified_at`
- `exclusive_equipment_verified_at`
- `meta_reviewed_at`

This prevents an old recommendation from making the entire hero profile look current.

## Official hero-system changes that matter to ranking

Official version history confirms several changes that make old hero guides risky to copy directly:

- hero skills and faction systems have been reworked over time;
- Exclusive Equipment was added for Orange heroes;
- Red Star and Hall of Honor systems were added later;
- lower-rarity Blue/Purple Exclusive Talents were rebalanced in 2025;
- hero index now includes season labels;
- Exclusive Equipment visuals and systems continue to receive updates.
