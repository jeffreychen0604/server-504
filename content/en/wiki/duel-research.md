# Duel Research

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Strong community consensus; exact costs and unlock requirements remain version-sensitive

**Duel Research** is the Alliance Duel-focused technology tree that improves how efficiently an account converts normal weekly activity into Alliance Duel points and rewards.

This tree is strategically unusual because improving it can increase the rate at which you earn the **Wisdom Medals** required for further advanced research.

## Frequently documented key nodes

Current community guides repeatedly reference the following Duel technologies:

- **Incentive Gathering** — increases points from gathering-related Duel tasks.
- **Incentive Zombies** — increases points from zombie-related Duel tasks.
- **Victory Tycoon** — broad Alliance Duel point multiplier.
- **Richer Rewards I** and **Richer Rewards II** — unlock additional personal milestone reward chests.
- **One More** — increases reward output at the end of the tree.

These names are strongly corroborated by multiple current community references, but exact percentages, prerequisite levels and Wisdom Medal costs should be read from the current in-game tree before being treated as Server 504 canonical data.

## Why Duel Research is often prioritized

The value loop is:

**Duel Research → easier/higher Alliance Duel scoring → more milestone rewards → more Wisdom Medals → faster advanced research**

For that reason, current community strategy generally recommends completing the important Duel multipliers before diverting a large Wisdom Medal reserve into Battle Strategy.

That recommendation is an optimization framework, not an official requirement. A combat-focused account may intentionally accept slower Duel progression to reach a critical Battle Strategy node.

## Be careful with old cost tables

Public reports show that Duel Research costs and presentation have changed across server generations. There are even player reports of different Richer Rewards paths/costs being visible in different interfaces on newer servers.

Because of this, the Wiki does **not** publish one historic Richer Rewards or One More cost as universal.

## Practical sequence

For a normal progressing account:

1. identify the next node that directly improves a recurring Duel theme;
2. prioritize milestone-unlock nodes such as Richer Rewards when reachable;
3. protect enough Wisdom Medals to avoid stalling immediately before a major node;
4. consume Medals during a matching event window when the timing is not urgent;
5. only shift heavily into Battle Strategy after the desired Duel efficiency layer is established.

## Alliance Duel changes can affect tree value

Official patches continue to change Alliance Duel itself — including Enemy Buster scheduling, Pet-item scoring and season-specific point acquisition. Therefore a Duel node's practical importance can change even when the node itself is unchanged.
