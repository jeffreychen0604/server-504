# Alliance Systems — Overview

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Core alliance mechanics. Server-specific diplomacy and allocation rules belong in the Server 504 Charter / Operational Codex, not in this article.

Alliances in Dark War: Survival are a progression, combat and administration layer rather than only a social group. Joining an active alliance improves account growth through **Alliance Help**, **Alliance Tech**, **Alliance Gifts**, coordinated rallies, reinforcements and alliance events.

## Core systems

### Alliance Help

Members can assist one another to reduce construction and research timers. The personal **Alliance Hall** controls how much alliance-help capacity and reinforcement capacity an account can receive.

### Alliance Tech

Alliance members donate to shared technology. Community guides consistently describe the tree as granting alliance-wide progression and combat bonuses such as construction/research efficiency and troop stats. Exact technology names, levels and numerical bonuses should be verified from current in-game UI before the Wiki publishes a full tree.

### Alliance Gifts

Alliance activity and spending can generate shared gifts. Gift progression also acts as a gate for some alliance event functionality: official patch notes confirm that at **Alliance Gifts Lv.20**, Bio-Mutant can run two Frankenstein rounds per event.

### Alliance administration

R4/R5 ranks receive operational permissions in multiple systems. Examples documented by current sources include Armory Assault registration, Allied Power alliance-task refresh, Bio-Mutant scheduling and other event initiations. Exact permissions vary by feature.

### Territory and structures

Alliance Centers, territory, cities, tiles and Strongholds appear across the world and seasonal conquest systems. Their attack, truce and protection rules are highly version- and season-dependent; the Wiki keeps those rules separate from permanent alliance mechanics.

## Game mechanic vs Server 504 policy

A crucial distinction:

- **Game mechanic:** what the game allows — registration limits, capture timers, rally/reinforcement rules, event permissions.
- **Server 504 policy:** how alliances voluntarily coordinate those mechanics — rotations, allocations, diplomatic limits, sanctions or shared-benefit agreements.

The Wiki documents the first. The Charter and Operational Codex document the second.
