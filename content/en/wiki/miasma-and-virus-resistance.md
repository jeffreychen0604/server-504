# Miasma & Virus Resistance

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for the existence of Virus Resistance, Miasma and official balance changes; medium for community-derived stack thresholds.

**Virus Resistance** is the main seasonal combat gate in Sealed Island. **Miasma** is the infection/penalty system that interacts with seasonal combat and shelter status.

## Virus Resistance

When your Virus Resistance is below the requirement shown for a seasonal target, combat efficiency drops. Community guides describe reduced damage and increased infection risk when attacking under the recommended resistance.

Official patch history confirms that the developers actively rebalance this layer. In November 2025 they:

- reduced the Viral Resistance requirement of Lv.65+ **Blood Moon Rotting Onis**;
- reduced the Viral Resistance requirement of Lv.65+ **Blood Moon Booze Brutes**.

Therefore, old resistance charts must not be treated as permanent.

## Where resistance comes from

The **Oni Seal Hall** is the main seasonal building associated with Virus Resistance in community references. Progressing it is usually more important than chasing raw CP when a resistance wall blocks new enemies or territory objectives.

## Miasma infection

Community guides consistently describe Miasma as a stack-based infection system. Sources report that:

- combat can add Miasma stacks;
- infected troops/shelters can suffer increasing penalties;
- alliance members can help with purification;
- natural purification also occurs over time;
- high stack counts can trigger severe shelter consequences.

Some guides publish exact thresholds such as 20/40/100 stacks and a destruction/teleport cycle. Those numbers are useful as a reference but are **not yet promoted to Server 504 canonical data** because they are not confirmed by official patch notes available to us.

## Miasma Bursts Alert

Official January patch notes confirm a **Miasma Bursts Alert** phase in Sealed Island. During this phase, the normal Miasma icon can be replaced by the **Oni King Statue** icon in the upper-left interface.

This confirms that Miasma is not only a passive debuff system; it also has scheduled/event-phase behavior.

## Practical play

Before fighting a new seasonal target:

1. check its Virus Resistance recommendation;
2. compare it with your current resistance;
3. upgrade Oni Seal Hall if the resistance gap is large;
4. monitor Miasma after repeated fights;
5. coordinate purification with alliance members when needed.

Do not use raw CP alone to judge whether a seasonal target is efficient to fight.
