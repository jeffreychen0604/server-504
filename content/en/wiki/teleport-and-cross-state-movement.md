# Teleport & Cross-State Movement

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for official current cross-state teleport behavior.

Teleport rules changed materially after launch, so older guides can be misleading.

## Current cross-state model

Official patch history states that, during supported cross-state events:

- teleporting no longer sends the survivor to a random location in the target State;
- the destination can be chosen directly;
- the opponent State map can be tapped to teleport during events such as **State of Supremacy** and **Alliance Duel — Enemy Buster**;
- **Advanced Teleporters** can be used inside the Lost Lands around the Capital;
- the old **Capital Teleporter** has been removed from the Alliance Shop;
- if a survivor still owns Capital Teleporters, those are consumed before Advanced Teleporters when entering the Lost Lands.

## Current world-map name colors

Official patches also changed map identification during cross-state activity:

- survivors from other States visible inside your Home State appear in red;
- during State of Supremacy, same-State troops/names are shown in yellow;
- other alliances from your own State may also use yellow in supported cross-state contexts;
- survivors not participating as the cross-state opponent can appear white.

Because these color rules have been revised more than once, use them as UI guidance rather than as a diplomacy rule.

## Same-State protection during State of Supremacy

Current official State of Supremacy rules prevent attacks on other survivors from the same State during the event, reducing accidental friendly attacks.

## Season-specific teleport rules

Conquest seasons can override ordinary movement. In Origin Lands, for example, official patches added teleporting onto tiles owned by the same camp/alliance and preserved tile ownership beneath the relocated shelter.

These rules must remain tagged to their season.

## Advanced Teleporter planning

The Alliance Shop weekly limit for Advanced Teleporters was officially increased from **1 to 4** in a 2026 patch. Shop values can change again, so the live shop remains the source of truth.
