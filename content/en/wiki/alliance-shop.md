# Alliance Shop

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for current 2026 community inventory categories; exact prices/limits remain UI-dependent.

The Alliance Shop is one of the most dependable free recurring exchange systems because its currency is tied to alliance activity rather than one short event.

## Currency

Recent 2026 community documentation calls the shop currency **Personal Points**. Players also commonly refer to the silver currency as Alliance Coins/Alliance Points.

Common ways to earn Personal Points include:

- Alliance Tech donations;
- Alliance Help activity;
- alliance events such as Zombie Siege;
- regular alliance participation.

## Current high-value stock categories

Recent 2026 sources list:

- Wisdom Medals;
- Orange Versatile Hero Fragments;
- Advanced Teleporters;
- Peace Shields;
- construction/research/training/general speedups;
- resource packs and Coins;
- selected lower-rarity hero fragments.

## Default purchase priorities

### 1. Wisdom Medals

After Duel/Battle Strategy research is unlocked and still incomplete, Wisdom Medals are usually one of the highest-value weekly purchases because they gate advanced research.

### 2. Orange Versatile Hero Fragments

These remain valuable because they advance Orange/S-rarity hero stars and therefore skill progression. Concentrating fragments into a long-term core hero is generally more efficient than spreading them across many heroes.

### 3. Advanced Teleporters and Peace Shields

These are not CP materials but can prevent much larger losses during war periods. Maintain a tactical reserve rather than treating them as emergency Ruby purchases.

### 4. Speedups

Useful when matched with Alliance Duel / Survival Preparedness / other scoring windows.

### 5. Basic resources

Usually last priority because they have more farmable sources.

## Important legacy warning

Recent 2026 Alliance Shop guides specifically warn that **Capital Teleporters are no longer available there**; Advanced Teleporters cover the relevant modern relocation use. Old shop checklists that still tell players to buy Capital Teleporters should be treated as outdated.

## Event-timing principle

Buying an item and **using** an item are separate decisions.

Examples:

- buy Wisdom Medals during the weekly reset, but spend them when research/event timing is favorable;
- buy Orange Versatile Fragments, then consume them during hero-star scoring if practical;
- store speedups for the matching construction/research/training objective.

Safety items are the exception: use shields/teleports immediately when needed.
