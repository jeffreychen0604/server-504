# Wisdom Medals

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** High for purpose/sources; exact event point values remain version-sensitive

**Wisdom Medals** are a scarce research material used primarily for advanced **Duel Research** and **Battle Strategy** progression.

Because these trees compete for the same resource pool, Wisdom Medals should be treated as a strategic currency rather than ordinary inventory.

## Confirmed / strongly supported uses

Current community references consistently identify Wisdom Medals as required for:

- **Duel Research**;
- **Battle Strategy**.

Official patch history confirms Battle Strategy as an active current Research Center branch, while current Alliance Shop material descriptions and guides continue to identify Wisdom Medals as the advanced research currency.

## Recurring sources

Current references list sources such as:

- Alliance Duel personal milestone rewards;
- Alliance Shop;
- legacy/current Honor-family shop sources depending rollout and version;
- selected event rewards / offers.

The exact weekly amount available varies with account progress, Duel Research, shop inventory and event version.

## Why hoarding can be correct

Spending a Wisdom Medal creates two kinds of value:

1. permanent research progression;
2. possible event points if the active theme rewards Medal consumption.

Therefore, if the research is not urgent, holding Medals until an overlapping scoring window can produce more total value.

Survival Preparedness has historically included Wisdom Medal consumption as a scoring action. Alliance Duel and other events can also make research timing more valuable. Always check the active event page because point values can change.

## Duel-first logic

A common current recommendation is:

**establish key Duel Research → improve future reward/Medal velocity → invest deeper into Battle Strategy.**

This is especially sensible when Richer Rewards / One More or other major Duel breakpoints are close.

Do not follow this blindly. If a critical combat technology is needed for a major war, Battle Strategy can reasonably take priority.

## Alliance Shop priority

Current 2026 community shop guidance usually ranks Wisdom Medals among the highest-value recurring Alliance Shop purchases after the relevant research trees unlock, because they gate permanent progression and have fewer routine sources than ordinary resources or speedups.

## Do not confuse with other medals

Dark War: Survival contains several medal-like resources. Pet progression, events and other systems can use their own medals/currencies. **Spec Ops Honor Medals**, for example, belong to Pet Agent progression and are not Wisdom Medals.
