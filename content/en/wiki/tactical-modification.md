# Tactical Modification

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High — unlock conditions and system purpose are explicitly documented in official patch notes.

**Tactical Modification** is an advanced Modified Vehicle progression layer designed to convert surplus vehicle-modification resources into additional squad combat power.

## Unlock conditions

Official October 2025 patch notes state that Tactical Modification unlocks when **any one** of the following conditions is met:

- Vehicle transformation / Modify level reaches **Lv.500**;
- **all Modified Vehicle parts reach Lv.66**;
- **any Orange vehicle chip reaches 10 stars**.

Only one route is required. This gives late-game accounts several ways to reach the system depending on where their APC investment is already strongest.

## Why this matters

The unlock conditions connect the three mature APC branches:

1. vehicle Modify progression;
2. six-part development;
3. Orange chip star progression.

A player approaching the threshold should therefore compare the marginal cost of each route rather than blindly pushing all three at once.

## Surplus-resource purpose

The official description specifically says Tactical Modification allows survivors to use **surplus vehicle modification items** to enhance squads. This indicates that the system is partly a late-game sink for materials that would otherwise lose value after reaching earlier caps.

Exact conversion rates, Tactical Modification levels and stat gains should be captured from the current in-game interface before being published as fixed numbers.

## Route-selection checklist

Before choosing an unlock path:

- If Modify is already near 500, finish the vehicle route.
- If all six parts are clustered near 66, the all-parts route may also complete Parts Set milestones on the way.
- If a core Orange chip is already highly starred, compare the cost of reaching 10★ against the other two paths.
- Do not sacrifice the primary formation's immediate combat performance just to unlock the system a few days earlier.
