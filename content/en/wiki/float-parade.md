# Float Parade

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** Medium. Community guides consistently place Float Parade inside the Sealed Island event cycle, but exact scoring/reward rules need current in-game verification.

**Float Parade** is a Sealed Island seasonal side event associated with the later Blood Moon / Oni escalation cycle.

Community references describe it as a lighter event phase compared with territory wars, Miasma management and major boss fighting.

## Role in the season

Float Parade appears as part of the broader sequence of seasonal activities rather than as a permanent progression system. Its practical value is therefore in:

- collecting seasonal rewards;
- completing limited missions or milestones;
- participating during otherwise lower-intensity windows;
- contributing to the season's event/currency economy.

Some community guides connect Float Parade with a **Kitsune King** procession and the transition toward later Sealed Island phases. Treat this as community terminology unless the same wording is visible in the current Server 504 interface.

## Practical checklist

When Float Parade appears:

1. read the current event rules before spending seasonal items;
2. check whether participation is time-windowed;
3. identify whether rewards are personal, alliance-wide or ranking-based;
4. confirm whether event currency expires at the end of the phase;
5. avoid assuming last week's route/stage/reward table will be identical if the event rotates.

## Why this article avoids hard numbers

Unlike Rotting Oni, Booze Brute and Oni King, Float Parade does not have easily accessible official patch-note entries that define its current scoring model. Public guides provide descriptions, but publishing their exact reward tables as canonical would weaken the Wiki's source standard.
