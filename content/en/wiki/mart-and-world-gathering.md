# Mart & World Gathering

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Mart building, gathering speed/load and world-map resource efficiency  
> **Confidence:** COMMUNITY for exact level values

The **Mart** is the shelter building associated with improving world-map gathering efficiency.

## Community-backed role

Current community references consistently describe the Mart as improving:

- **Gathering Speed**;
- **Gathering Load**;
- with Rubies excluded from the gathering-speed effect in older tables.

This makes Mart value most visible when your account spends meaningful march time gathering Wood, Iron, Electricity or other world resources.

## Why exact tables are not locked

Public Mart tables disagree in presentation and some older pages contain values that appear to be incremental rather than total bonuses. Account bonuses can also make screenshots misleading if the UI is not showing base values.

For this reason the Wiki does not publish a permanent Lv.1–30 calculator yet.

## Practical priority

Mart is usually a throughput building, not an emergency gate.

Upgrade it more aggressively when:

- resource gathering is a real progression bottleneck;
- multiple marches spend long periods on resource tiles;
- you are entering long Industrial upgrade cycles;
- gathering events reward time on tiles or gathered volume.

Delay it relative to hard prerequisites when Watchtower, Research Center, Alliance Hall or military buildings are blocking progression.

## Related systems

- Gathering Ground affects Exploration rather than world-resource gathering.
- Research and Alliance Tech can also improve gathering performance.
- Hero skills may affect resource economy without being Mart effects.
