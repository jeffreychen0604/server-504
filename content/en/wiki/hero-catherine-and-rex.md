# Catherine & Rex

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Fighter  
> **Specialty:** Radar

Catherine & Rex are a two-in-one Fighter hero whose defining value is **Radar progression** rather than pure faction combat.

## Acquisition

Community data lists:

- **Legend Battle Pass**;
- selected special offers.

## Skill structure

### Go Rex Go! — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill.

### Shred — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Sharp Senses — Progression Skill

Public data lists:

- free Radar events per day;
- increased Radar food yield;
- additional free task quantity and stronger Radar yield bonuses through star progression.

This creates persistent account value because Radar activity feeds progression even when Catherine & Rex are not used in the strongest current combat formation.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Progression utility / Radar economy.**

Best-fit use cases:

- active Radar players;
- accounts still relying on Radar for food, tasks and progression materials;
- early/mid account development;
- secondary Fighter formation when their existing stars make replacement inefficient.
