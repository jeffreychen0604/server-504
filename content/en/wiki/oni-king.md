# Oni King

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for official boss existence and HP rebalance; medium for community phase/lore details.

The **Oni King** is the major boss encounter associated with Sealed Island's late-season escalation.

## Official balance history

Official November 2025 patch notes state that the Oni King's HP was increased because some servers defeated it too quickly for other survivors to participate. The developers explicitly said the goal was to extend participation time **without increasing encounter difficulty**.

This distinction matters:

- boss **HP** can change;
- boss **difficulty** does not necessarily change at the same time;
- old kill-time expectations therefore become unreliable after a patch.

## Miasma Bursts connection

A later official patch confirms that during the **Miasma Bursts Alert** phase, the Miasma icon in the top-left interface can be replaced by the **Oni King Statue** icon.

That indicates a direct UI/event-state connection between Miasma escalation and Oni King activity.

## Practical alliance preparation

Before the Oni King phase:

1. announce the expected start time in server time;
2. make sure players have sufficient Virus Resistance for the active phase;
3. keep strongest rally leads available;
4. avoid unnecessary troop commitments immediately before the boss window;
5. coordinate participation so the boss is not burned down before late arrivals can score if participation rewards matter;
6. verify any personal attack-count, damage-ranking or milestone rules in the current event UI.

## What not to publish as permanent truth

Do not hard-code:

- Oni King HP;
- recommended CP;
- Virus Resistance requirement;
- number of attempts;
- damage milestones;
- ranking reward values.

Official patch history already proves the boss HP was changed after launch.

## Community naming note

Some guides connect the Oni King to Shuten-dōji / Shuten-doom terminology. These names are useful for finding community guides, but the Server 504 Wiki should prefer names visible in the current Dark War: Survival UI when documenting mechanics.
