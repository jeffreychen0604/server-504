# Game Overview

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for publisher/platform/current feature names; community-verified for deeper mechanics.

Dark War: Survival is a mobile strategy game published by **Florere Game Limited**. The official store description frames the game around rebuilding a shelter after a zombie-virus collapse, developing survivors and troops, joining alliances, and competing on a shared world map.

## Core gameplay loop

The long-term game loop combines several systems rather than a single progression track:

1. **Shelter development** — upgrade the Watchtower and prerequisite buildings, maintain resource production, research technology and train units.
2. **Hero development** — recruit heroes, raise levels and stars, improve skills and equipment, and build formations around faction synergy.
3. **Troop development** — train Fighter, Rider and Shooter units and unlock higher tiers as the shelter progresses.
4. **APC / Modified Vehicle development** — improve the vehicle, parts and chips that support formations on the world map.
5. **Alliance play** — rallies, alliance research, territory activity, alliance events and cross-state competition are major sources of progress and rewards.
6. **Seasonal systems** — seasons add temporary buildings, resistance mechanics, currencies, monsters and territory objectives.
7. **Recurring events** — progression resources are frequently tied to timed events such as Alliance Duel, Survival Preparedness, State of Supremacy, Zombie Siege and Bio-Mutant.

## Current-era systems worth knowing

Official patch notes confirm that the game has continued to expand well beyond the original launch systems. Examples include:

- **Industrial Age** progression beyond the normal Watchtower era.
- **Modified Vehicle** parts, chips, set bonuses and Tactical Modification.
- **Wonders** (the feature previously called Decorations) and Wonder Gallery management.
- **Pet Agents / Special Ops Outpost**, including rescue, training and growth systems.
- **Fierce March**, a VIP 12 command that can automatically engage enemy troops encountered along a march path.

Because systems change over time, Server 504 Wiki should prefer dated patch information for hard limits and unlock conditions instead of treating old community tables as permanent truth.
