# Blood Moon Shroud

> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for event existence and official conflict handling; medium for community reward/currency details.

**Blood Moon Shroud** is a Sealed Island event phase tied to corrupted seasonal enemies and the broader Miasma cycle.

Official patch notes explicitly reference Blood Moon versions of both **Rotting Oni** and **Booze Brute**, and later added a warning when **Zombie Siege** overlaps with Blood Moon Shroud.

## What changes during Blood Moon

Community references describe Blood Moon as an escalation phase where stronger corrupted targets appear and seasonal currencies/rewards become available.

The exact reward table and monster thresholds are version-sensitive. Official balance notes already show that the developers reduced Viral Resistance requirements for high-level Blood Moon enemies after launch.

## Event conflict handling

Official October 2025 notes added a secondary confirmation prompt when **Zombie Siege** conflicts with **Blood Moon Shroud**.

Operationally, this means alliance leadership should avoid assuming both events can be run casually at the same time. When schedules overlap, players may need a clear priority announcement.

## Magatama and exchange content

Community guides associate Blood Moon activity with **Magatama** and a limited exchange shop often referred to as **Lan's Shadow Shop**. They also report Rotting Oni and Booze Brute as major sources of this seasonal currency.

Because shop stock, costs and reset behavior can change, the Server 504 Wiki should treat any exact exchange table as a dated snapshot rather than a permanent list.

## Practical checklist

Before Blood Moon Shroud:

- confirm your Virus Resistance;
- prepare stamina/AP if needed for repeated monster hunting;
- check alliance rally timing for Booze Brutes;
- verify whether another alliance event overlaps;
- review the active seasonal shop before spending Magatama;
- avoid carrying large amounts of event-only currency into the final phase without checking expiry rules.
