# Rally, Reinforcement & Defense

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current Capital/Turret and Rally Restriction features; medium for situational community observations.

Rallies and reinforcements are alliance-level combat tools. Their current behavior has changed through patches, so old “one rally only” or older capacity advice can be wrong.

## Rally

A rally is an alliance attack initiated by one player and joined by other members. The initiator selects the target and can control rally restrictions when the feature is available.

Official 2026 world-battle notes confirm **Rally Restriction** by unit type. The game also saves those restriction settings based on the troop, making faction-specific rally presets easier to reuse.

### Why restrictions matter

If a rally strategy is built around Fighter, Rider or Shooter buffs, allowing every faction to join can weaken synergy. Restriction is therefore an operational tool, not merely a convenience setting.

The exact combat contribution of the rally initiator versus joiners should be verified from battle reports before the Wiki publishes a universal “rally lead stats determine everything” rule. Community discussions often simplify this mechanic too aggressively.

## Capital and Turret changes

Official December 2025 notes changed Capital/Turret warfare substantially:

- rally limits for the **Capital** and **Turrets** were removed;
- reinforcement limits for those targets were removed;
- survivors can launch multiple rallies and send multiple reinforcement squads to the targets separately;
- launching a rally or sending reinforcements to a Capital/Turret receives an additional **40,000 troop capacity**.

These rules supersede older guides written before that patch.

## Reinforcement

Reinforcement sends a formation to defend another shelter or structure. It is used in alliance wars, Zombie Siege, territory defense and Capital/Turret control.

Community observations report that reinforcement travel can be faster inside controlled alliance territory than across uncaptured territory. Treat this as a map/territory behavior to verify in the live server rather than a guaranteed fixed multiplier.

## City Gate defense

Official updates added **Defense Presets**:

- multiple defense squads can be prepared in advance;
- if heroes in the first preset are busy, the next preset is used;
- if no fallback preset exists, the strongest available heroes are auto-deployed.

Before major combat, check that your defense formation is not accidentally dependent on heroes currently committed to a rally, gather or remote march.

## Bio-Mutant special case

Official 2026 notes introduced a special rule for Bio-Mutant: if the rally initiator's shelter is within **20 tiles** of Frankenstein, the rally force's march time is fixed at **3 seconds**. This is an event-specific mechanic and should not be generalized to normal PvP rallies.

## Server 504 operational checklist

For important rallies:

1. rally lead announces target and required faction;
2. check Rally Restriction before launch;
3. joiners use the requested unit type and avoid underfilled/incorrect troops;
4. do not consume heroes needed for a critical defense preset unless ordered;
5. inspect the report after the fight and adjust based on actual buff gaps.
