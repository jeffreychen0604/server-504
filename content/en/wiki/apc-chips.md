# APC Chips

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for Chip System existence, official reward sources, star progression and formation integration; medium for exact crafting recipes.

The **Chip System** is a later Modified Vehicle layer that lets survivors **craft and install chips** to increase APC combat power.

## Official launch behavior

Official September 2025 patch notes introduced the Chip System and confirmed several recurring sources:

- **Chip Materials** from the 3rd, 6th and 9th point-tier chests in Daily Alliance Duel;
- **Random Blue Chip Chests** from Zombie Siege individual and alliance ranking rewards;
- the **Chip Chrono** event;
- later Mod Vehicle packs containing Purple or Green Chip Chests.

Reward pools can change, so these are system/source references rather than a guaranteed permanent shopping list.

## Craft and install

The developer explicitly describes the system as **craft and install chips** for the Modified Vehicle. However, public official notes do not document the full crafting recipe, menu unlock requirement or every rarity conversion step.

For Server 504 Wiki, do not publish an old recipe as current truth unless it is verified from the current Chip Factory interface.

## Faction-aware chips

Official updates show that chips are connected to troop composition:

- Defense formations can automatically match the best chip setup based on unit numbers and unit types.
- Later UI updates added faction indicators to chip icons.
- Champion Duel fixes referenced chip bonuses syncing with formation changes.

This means a chip setup should be treated as part of a formation, not as a separate “highest CP chip wins” system.

## Star progression

Official patch notes refer directly to **Chip star upgrades** and later use **an Orange vehicle chip reaching 10 stars** as one of the unlock conditions for Tactical Modification.

Therefore, chip progression has at least three strategically important layers:

1. chip rarity;
2. chip star level;
3. compatibility with the active formation/faction.

## Practical priority

For a developing main APC:

- collect recurring free Chip Materials before buying into the system;
- build chips that support the primary troop faction;
- check active chip setup whenever changing formation composition;
- do not consume rare chips blindly if they can later support star progression or Tactical Modification unlocks.
