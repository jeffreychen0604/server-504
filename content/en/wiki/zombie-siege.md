# Zombie Siege

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current duration/interval and scoring-rule changes; medium for optimal reinforcement tactics because alliance strength differs.

**Zombie Siege** is an alliance-defense event in which waves of zombies attack member shelters. Personal and alliance performance depend on surviving waves and using reinforcements efficiently.

## Core rules

Community event data describes the main structure as follows:

- R4/R5 can start the event;
- zombies attack alliance members in waves;
- a member stops receiving attacks after the shelter is breached twice;
- successful defenses generate Defense Points;
- reinforcing allies can also generate points;
- personal and alliance ranking rewards are settled after the event.

## Current pacing

Official August 2025 patch notes significantly shortened the event:

- duration changed from **62.5 minutes to 37.5 minutes**;
- zombie attack interval changed from **150 seconds to 90 seconds**;
- zombie attack power increased while zombie HP decreased, with overall strength intended to remain similar;
- zombies now attack from the territory closest to the alliance assembly point.

Any old guide built around the 62.5-minute pacing is therefore outdated.

## Current Defense Point rule

Official December 2025 notes changed an important scoring detail.

Previously, a shelter with no garrisoned troops could fail to receive Defense Points even if the defense succeeded. Under the newer rule, the shelter owner earns Defense Points as long as the shelter successfully defends.

Reinforcing squads continue to earn points according to their kill contribution.

## Practical defense model

A safe baseline strategy is:

1. keep the strongest main formation protecting your own shelter as waves become dangerous;
2. use spare marches to reinforce members who can still survive later waves;
3. monitor wounded troops and hospital load between waves;
4. recall damaged reinforcements when needed, heal, then redeploy;
5. after your own shelter has been eliminated from further waves, reinforce surviving stronger shelters to continue contributing.

Community discussion commonly recommends keeping your strongest march at home in difficult phases while using secondary marches for reinforcement points.

## Reinforcement priorities

Do not reinforce randomly. Useful priorities are:

- members whose shelters are still active in the event;
- high-survivability members likely to survive deeper waves;
- alliance-defined gate/tower/critical positions where applicable;
- targets with space for useful troops rather than already-full low-efficiency garrisons.

## Before the event

Alliance leadership should:

- announce the event start time;
- ensure members are online where possible;
- remind members to heal before start;
- define whether spare marches should reinforce weaker or stronger members first;
- identify high-survivability shelters for late-wave reinforcement;
- ensure new players understand that fighting zombies outside the shelter is not the same as defending Siege waves.

## Rewards and material value

Community item data lists **Titanium Alloy** among materials obtainable from Zombie Siege, making the event relevant to APC progression as well as alliance rankings. Reward tables can change, so the current event reward panel should be treated as authoritative.
