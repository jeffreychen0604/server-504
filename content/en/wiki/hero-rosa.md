# Rosa

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Season:** 4  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Shooter  
> **Specialty:** Shooter damage / counterattack mitigation

Rosa is a Shooter combat hero whose kit combines **Shooter damage scaling** with reduced counterattack damage taken.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Rosa as a **Legend Battle Pass** hero.

## Skill structure

### Blade Storm — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero pattern.

### Candy Chainsaw — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Rainbow Shield — Passive Skill

Public data describes two layers:

- increased Shooter combat damage;
- progressive reduction to **counterattack damage taken** by Rosa's troop.

This gives Rosa a useful mixed identity: offense remains the primary purpose, but part of her value comes from reducing punishment during sustained exchanges.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Shooter combat / high when already developed.**

Rosa is most relevant when:

- Shooter is the main faction;
- the account wants offensive scaling without giving up all defensive utility;
- counterattack damage is a meaningful source of losses in combat reports;
- her stars are already ahead of competing Shooter replacements.

Because Rosa is Battle Pass sourced, replacement cost can be significant. Compare actual report output and losses before moving investment away from a mature Rosa.
