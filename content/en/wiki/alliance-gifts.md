# Alliance Gifts

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for the system's existence and the Lv.20 Bio-Mutant gate; exact gift XP/reward tables are not yet canonical.

Alliance Gifts are shared alliance rewards generated through alliance activity and purchases. They are one of the reasons active alliances accelerate account growth even for members who are not the ones spending.

## What Alliance Gifts provide

Community sources consistently describe Alliance Gifts as a source of free rewards such as resources, speedups and premium-value items. Exact gift contents depend on gift type and current game version.

## Alliance Gifts level

Alliance Gifts also have a progression level. This is not merely cosmetic: official patch notes confirm a direct gameplay gate at **Alliance Gifts Lv.20**.

At Lv.20 or higher, an alliance can activate **two Frankenstein rounds** during one Bio-Mutant event.

Official conditions include:

- round 2 must start at least 10 minutes after round 1 ends;
- each survivor may participate in only one round per event;
- each survivor receives rewards only once;
- registering for both rounds delays reward distribution until the second round concludes.

## Operational implication

Alliance Gifts level therefore affects alliance event flexibility. A higher-level alliance can split members across separate Frankenstein windows, which helps alliances spanning multiple time zones.
