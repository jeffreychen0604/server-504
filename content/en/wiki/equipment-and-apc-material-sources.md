# Equipment & APC Material Sources

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Hero-equipment and Modified Vehicle/APC materials only.  
> **Rule:** Separate materials by system so similarly named items are not mixed.

Dark War: Survival has several high-value materials that look similar in inventory but belong to completely different progression systems.

## Hero equipment track

### Boost Ores

Used to enhance hero equipment through the normal enhancement track.

Legacy community sources list:

- Armory Assault;
- Bio-Mutant;
- seasonal Equipment Battle Pass;
- VIP Shop;
- Season Shop;
- Trial Shop;
- legacy Capital / Black Gold shop sources;
- selected offers.

Because Boost Ores have several repeatable sources, they are usually less strategically scarce than Power Cores or DX Blueprints.

### Power Cores

Used after Orange equipment reaches the relevant enhancement gate. Public item data describes Orange equipment becoming eligible for Power Core upgrades after enhancement Lv.20.

Source families include:

- Season Shop;
- VIP Shop;
- Honor-family / legacy Capital-Arena-Black Gold sources;
- Trial Shop;
- Total War / other milestone events;
- Bio-Mutant individual rewards;
- offers.

### DX Blueprints

DX Blueprints belong to **late hero-equipment / red-equipment breakthrough**, not the APC.

Recent 2026 community guides report them in the Honor Shop and other high-end reward routes. Exact prices/weekly limits should be confirmed in Server 504.

## APC / Modified Vehicle track

### Titanium Alloy

Used to enhance APC parts.

Documented source families include:

- Capital Clash participation/ranking rewards;
- Zombie Siege;
- Season Shop;
- legacy Black Gold / Honor-family shop sources;
- selected offers.

### Design Blueprint

Used for advanced APC part progression after the early part levels.

Legacy item data lists:

- Season Shop;
- Black Gold Shop (now a legacy Honor-family reference where the merger is active);
- selected offers.

**Design Blueprint and DX Blueprint are different items.**

### Vehicle Parts / Gears / chip materials

The Modified Vehicle system has been changed by later patches, including Parts Sets, chips and Tactical Modification. Older APC material charts can therefore become structurally outdated even when the material name is still correct.

Use the current APC article for the modern system architecture and treat older exact recipes as verification targets.

## Quick decision table

| Material | System | Spend when... | Avoid when... |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boost Ores | Hero equipment | Main formation has equipment worth enhancing | You are dumping them into disposable lower-tier gear |
| Power Cores | Orange hero equipment | Orange gear has reached the Power Core gate and the formation is long-term | The gear/hero is temporary or event timing is poor |
| DX Blueprint | Red hero equipment | A real red breakthrough is reachable | You are months away from the prerequisite gear state |
| Titanium Alloy | APC parts | The chosen APC part path is defined | You are upgrading randomly because the button is available |
| Design Blueprint | APC parts | Later APC recipe explicitly requires it | You confused it with DX Blueprint |

## Source-history warning

Several 2025 item pages name Capital Shop, Arena Shop and Black Gold Shop independently. In 2026 the developer announced their consolidation into Honor Shop with Honor Points. The item may still be available, but the **shop label in an old source can be obsolete**.
