# Trade Post & Merchant Guild Dark Zones

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for official dark-zone combat modifiers; broader structure ownership/economy mechanics still need current capture.

Trade Post and Merchant Guild areas can create **dark zones** where shelter combat behaves differently from ordinary world-map attacks.

## Official dark-zone combat modifiers

A December 2025 official patch changed shelter attacks inside a Trade Post / Merchant Guild dark zone:

- attacking-troop mortality rate: **20% → 10%**;
- defending troops receive an **additional +4% mortality rate**;
- shelter durability reduction multiplier was increased by **500%**.

This means a shelter that looks defensible by normal-map standards may be much more vulnerable to durability pressure inside one of these zones.

## Why positioning matters

The relevant lesson is not simply "attackers are safer." Dark-zone combat changes several loss channels at the same time:

1. attacker mortality;
2. defender mortality;
3. shelter durability damage.

Always identify the map zone before comparing two battle reports.

## Interaction with world battles

Current official client controls allow troops to be dragged directly onto world-map targets including **Merchant Guild** structures. The exact interaction options depend on the current event/season state.

## What is not yet canonical

The public patch history confirms dark-zone combat modifiers, but it does not provide a complete current specification for:

- who may occupy/control each Trade Post / Merchant Guild;
- reward cadence;
- structure level progression;
- dark-zone radius;
- all capture conditions;
- whether those values vary by season/state progression.

Those fields should be filled from Server 504 in-game screenshots, not inferred from the dark-zone patch alone.
