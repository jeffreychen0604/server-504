Guy is a Fighter hero whose strongest long-term identity is **Adventure utility**, even though one structured field in the same community source labels him Combat.

## Acquisition

Community data lists several acquisition paths:

- Radio Station recruitment;
- selected Radar missions for fragments;
- the early **first top-up** offer.

Availability can change by account age and event cycle.

## Skill structure

### Fire Barrage — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero combat pattern.

### Wind Blade — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Samurai's Gift — progression / Adventure utility

This is Guy's defining skill. Public data lists benefits that include:

- increased idle Food gain from Adventure;
- increased Energy recovery speed at later star breakpoints;
- free daily Energy at selected star breakpoints.

Because these effects function outside pure PvP damage, Guy can retain account value even after stronger Fighter combat heroes replace him in the main march.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Progression utility / preserve.**

Guy is most useful for:

- Adventure-focused progression;
- passive Food income;
- Energy recovery and daily Energy utility;
- early and mid-game Fighter formations when roster depth is limited.

Do not judge Guy only by main-march combat strength. His replacement cost includes the Adventure economy he provides.
