# Hero Skills & Passives

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for the skill categories shown on established hero data; medium for individual values because heroes are patched over time.

Hero value in Dark War: Survival is not determined by displayed CP alone. A hero can contribute direct damage, faction-wide combat buffs, troop capacity, economy, training, research or event scoring depending on the skill set.

## Common skill structure

Established hero databases repeatedly show four broad skill/talent layers on heroes:

1. **Active Skill** — the hero's main combat skill.
2. **Normal Attack** — repeated direct damage between major skill activations.
3. **Passive / Progression Skill** — varies widely by hero. It may buff a faction, improve training/research/resources, increase event scoring or support another account system.
4. **Exclusive Talent** — an additional hero/faction-oriented attribute layer.

The third slot is especially important to read carefully. It is not always a combat passive. For example, some heroes improve faction ATK/DEF/HP or battle damage while others specialize in training, research, gathering or Alliance Duel progression.

## Faction passives

Combat heroes can provide bonuses to Fighter, Rider or Shooter troops. These bonuses are valuable because they can scale an entire formation rather than only the hero that owns the skill.

Examples documented by community databases include heroes whose passives increase:

- faction ATK;
- faction DEF;
- faction HP;
- battle damage;
- normal-attack damage reduction;
- skill damage;
- troop capacity.

Exact percentages should live on individual hero pages with a verification date.

## Blue and purple hero talent update

Official September 2025 patch notes changed several lower-rarity heroes' exclusive talents so they remain relevant in developed accounts. The update added effects such as:

- troop capacity increases;
- ATK for in-troop Fighter / Shooter / Rider heroes;
- DEF for in-troop Fighter / Shooter / Rider heroes.

This confirms an important Wiki rule: rarity alone does not tell the whole story, and old hero descriptions can become obsolete after talent reworks.

## Upgrade priority

Do not use a universal rule such as “always max Active first” for every hero. A better process is:

1. identify the hero's real job in the formation;
2. determine whether the key value is combat, faction support or progression utility;
3. compare the next skill level to the resource cost;
4. prioritize the main formation before secondary heroes;
5. re-check after unlocking Exclusive Equipment, because it can change a hero's practical value.
