# Black Market

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Daily Black Market, manual refresh and time-limited Black Market  
> **Confidence:** HIGH for refresh/Quick Buy behavior; inventory and discounts remain rotating

The **Black Market** is a rotating discount shop for account-growth items.

## Officially confirmed behavior

Apple's Dark War: Survival editorial confirms that the Black Market can offer:

- resources;
- speedups;
- recruitment tickets;
- other discounted items.

The editorial also states that offers refresh daily and can be refreshed manually with Rubies. Repeated refreshes can increase the chance of higher-value offers.

A later official patch added **Quick Buy** to the **time-limited Black Market**, allowing purchases without a confirmation pop-up when the function is enabled.

## Purchase discipline

Black Market value is contextual. A discount is not automatically a good purchase.

Prioritize items that remove the account's current bottleneck:

- rare progression materials;
- high-value speedups when an event/upgrade is actually planned;
- recruitment resources when the current pool is useful;
- materials that are difficult to farm elsewhere.

Do not burn Rubies refreshing the market simply because the current page looks weak. Refreshing has an opportunity cost against teleports, event items and other Ruby uses.

## What the Wiki does not freeze

The following are **not** treated as permanent:

- exact item pool;
- discount percentages;
- refresh price progression;
- daily refresh count;
- time-limited Black Market inventory.

Those values should come from current Server 504 UI captures.
