# Shops & Currencies Overview

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Free exchange shops, season/event shops and current-vs-legacy currency naming.  
> **Rule:** Shop inventory and purchase limits are version-sensitive. Server 504 in-game UI overrides public guides.

Dark War: Survival has several exchange systems that use different currencies and reset schedules. The most important change for reading older guides is that **Capital Shop, Black Gold Shop and Arena Shop are being consolidated into a unified Honor Shop**.

## Current shop map

| Shop / exchange | Main purpose | Currency | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Honor Shop | Rare combat/equipment materials | Honor Points | Officially introduced; rollout was staged |
| Alliance Shop | Research, hero fragments, safety and utility items | Personal Points / Alliance currency | Current community-verified |
| Season Shop | Season-limited progression, skins and upgrade materials | Season-specific currency/points | Rotates by season |
| VIP Shop | VIP-gated recurring materials and utility items | Verify current UI | Current; stock varies by VIP/state |
| Combat Shop | Combat-earned exchange items, including equipment references | Mode-specific currency | Still referenced in recent 2026 guides |
| Trial Shop | Trial-mode rewards / lucky equipment references | Mode-specific currency | Still referenced in recent 2026 guides |
| Trade Shop / Merchant Guild | Trading and Merchant Guild systems | Feature-specific | Officially expanded in 2026 |
| Event exchange shops | Temporary event rewards | Event-specific currency | Temporary; do not carry rules across events |

## The major 2026 shop migration

Official update notes announced:

- **Capital Shop + Black Gold Shop + Arena Shop → Honor Shop**;
- currencies from **State of Supremacy, Capital Clash and Black Gold Battlefield → Honor Points**;
- **1 Capital Glory Badge → 40 Honor Points**;
- **1 Black Gold Coin → 2 Honor Points**.

The rollout was initially announced for selected early States. Therefore, an older source saying “buy this from Capital Shop” may still describe the correct *acquisition family* but use a legacy shop name.

## How this Wiki handles old shop references

When an item database says:

- `Capital Shop`,
- `Black Gold Shop`, or
- `Arena Shop`,

this Wiki treats the source as **legacy naming** and checks whether the item now belongs to the Honor Shop in the current Server 504 UI.

The old source is not deleted because it still helps identify where the item historically entered the economy.

## Purchase-priority rule

Shop priority should follow the account bottleneck, not a permanent tier list.

A useful default order is:

1. scarce permanent progression material;
2. main-formation star/equipment breakpoint;
3. Industrial/APC bottleneck;
4. war utility that prevents losses (shield/teleport);
5. event-timed speedups;
6. farmable resources last.

Late-game accounts may reverse some priorities once a research tree or equipment tier is complete.

## Version warning

Do not copy exact prices, weekly limits or stock tables into a permanent guide without a current screenshot. The developer has repeatedly merged shops, changed packs and altered event currencies.
