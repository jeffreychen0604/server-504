# Honor Shop — Price & Limit Audit

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for the shop merger and legacy-currency conversion; current item prices/weekly limits require Server 504 verification.

For the stable current shop model, use [Honor Shop](#/wiki/honor-shop) and [Shops & Currencies Overview](#/wiki/shops-and-currencies-overview). This page exists specifically to audit price/limit claims that can drift after rollout or tuning; the [Resource Calculators](#/wiki/wiki-calculators) only uses the official historical currency-conversion formula.

Honor Shop replaced three older shop surfaces:

- Capital Shop
- Black Gold Shop
- Arena Shop

Official patch notes state that currencies from State of Supremacy, Capital Clash and Black Gold Battlefield were replaced by **Honor Points**.

## Official conversion

At the migration:

- **1 Capital Glory Badge → 40 Honor Points**
- **1 Black Gold Coin → 2 Honor Points**

These ratios are safe to use in the calculator as historical conversion math.

## Why the Wiki does not publish a current price table yet

Community discussions show that Honor Shop values were adjusted after launch — including Precision Parts pricing. That makes screenshots from a different State or month unreliable for current weekly purchase planning.

The current Wiki therefore separates:

### Safe to publish

- shop identity and merged legacy names;
- Honor Points as the unified currency;
- official legacy conversion ratios;
- classes of rare items historically associated with the Honor family of shops.

### Needs Server 504 capture

- current price of Precision Parts;
- DX Blueprint price and weekly limit;
- Design Blueprint price and weekly limit;
- Orange Hero Fragment price and weekly limit;
- Orange equipment availability and price;
- Power Core price and limit;
- any State-age or VIP gating.

## Purchase-priority guidance

Community advice often prioritizes hard-to-source items such as DX Blueprint, Design Blueprint and late-game equipment over materials available cheaply elsewhere. This is **strategy opinion**, not a fixed shop rule.

The correct decision depends on:

1. the current Server 504 price;
2. weekly cap;
3. the player's bottleneck;
4. whether the same item is cheaper in Alliance/VIP/Season/Trial sources.
