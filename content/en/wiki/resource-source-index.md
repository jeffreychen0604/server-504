## Equipment-source interpretation

### Power Cores

A 2025 item database lists Season, VIP, Capital, Arena, Trial and Black Gold shops plus Total War/Bio-Mutant rewards. Because Capital/Arena/Black Gold are affected by the 2026 Honor merger, do not assume all six separate shops still exist on Server 504.

### Boost Ores

Legacy source lists include Armory Assault, Bio-Mutant, seasonal Equipment Battle Pass, VIP Shop, Season Shop, Capital/Trial/Black Gold shops and offers.

### DX vs Design Blueprint

- **Design Blueprint** → APC / Modified Vehicle parts.
- **DX Blueprint** → late hero-equipment/red-equipment progression.

Never merge these into one database field called simply “Blueprint”.

## Industrial-source interpretation

Precision Parts are documented through **Industrial Surge**: complete Radar events, defeat Steel Zombies, obtain Mechanical Components, then exchange them for Precision Parts and other materials.

Official Jan 2026 patch notes also increased the purchase limit of a Precision Parts Pack, confirming that paid pack availability can change independently of free acquisition routes.

## Source-confidence labels

- **Official/current:** direct patch note or official service page.
- **Recent community:** 2026 guide consistent with current systems.
- **Legacy community:** older item database useful for source history but may use renamed shops.
- **Server 504 verification:** use current in-game UI when shop naming, price or purchase limit matters.
