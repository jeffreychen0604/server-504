# Marcia

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Rider HP / Rider battle damage

Marcia is a Rider combat hero built around **durability plus battle-damage scaling**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Marcia from:

- Elite Recruitment Tickets in the Radio Station;
- the Goodie Bazaar event.

## Skill structure

### Cluster-Annihilation — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero scaling pattern.

### Infusion – Penetration — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Shadow of Gale — Passive Skill

Public data describes Marcia as increasing Riders' HP, with star progression adding **Rider battle damage**.

This gives her a simple but useful formation identity: strengthen the march's health pool while improving total faction combat output.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Main/secondary Rider combat / depends on star depth.**

Marcia is most useful when:

- Rider is the account's primary faction;
- the formation needs more HP without giving up battle-damage scaling;
- she is already substantially starred;
- a second Rider formation needs a durable anchor.

She has no separate economy utility, so her long-term value should be judged against the actual combat heroes available on the account rather than kept automatically.
