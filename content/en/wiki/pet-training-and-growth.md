# Pet Training & Growth

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Official for training/growth as core systems; community-backed for attribute refinement behavior and optimization strategy.

Pet growth has two different layers that should not be confused:

1. **level / breakthrough progression**;
2. **training / attribute refinement**.

Official patch notes confirm training and growth as core Pet Agent features, but do not publish the detailed formulas.

## Level progression

Community guides describe Pet Agents as following level milestones that unlock later Agents and stronger progression tiers.

A common low-spender strategy is to stop investing heavily in early low-rarity Agents after they reach the level needed for the next unlock, then preserve rarer materials for later A/S-tier Agents.

This is a **strategy recommendation**, not an official rule.

## Special Training / Attribute Refinement

A current 2026 community guide documents an important UI behavior:

- **Train** rolls a new set of attributes;
- the rolled attributes are not automatically committed;
- **Replace** is required to accept the new roll.

Because reroll UI is version-sensitive, the exact button wording should still be checked against Server 504's current English UI.

### Safe workflow

1. Note your current saved stats before training.
2. Tap Train once.
3. Compare the proposed roll.
4. If you want it, confirm it with Replace/accept before rolling again.
5. Never assume a green CP number automatically means the roll was saved.

## Attribute quality

Community guidance emphasizes that raw CP is not always the best decision signal.

Commonly valued development rolls include:

- Building Speed;
- Research Speed.

Commonly valued late-game combat rolls include:

- Total Army ATK;
- Total Army HP;
- faction/unit damage-type bonuses.

Exact attribute pools, percentages and rarity bands are not yet canonical in this Wiki.

## Event timing

Officially, consuming Pet items can score in:

- Alliance Duel — Holistic Growth;
- Survival Preparedness — Unit Training.

This makes Pet leveling and training a strong candidate for double-dip planning rather than daily uncontrolled spending.
