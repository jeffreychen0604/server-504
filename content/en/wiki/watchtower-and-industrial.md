# Watchtower & Industrial Progression

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for the progression model; medium for exact old resource tables because requirements can be adjusted.

The **Watchtower** is the main shelter progression gate. Advancing it usually requires both the Watchtower's own resources and prerequisite buildings at specific levels.

## What actually slows progression

Community progression tables show two recurring blockers through the higher Watchtower levels:

- **Alliance Hall** appears repeatedly as a prerequisite.
- **Research Center** is another recurring prerequisite.

Players who upgrade only the Watchtower-target resources but neglect these prerequisite buildings can still be blocked from starting the next level.

## Resource transition

Early Watchtower levels rely mainly on basic shelter resources. Around the mid-game, additional resources become part of the upgrade path and costs increase sharply. Exact costs should be treated as versioned data rather than permanent constants.

## Industrial Age

Normal building progression eventually transitions into the **Industrial Age**, where selected buildings can advance beyond their normal level cap using **Precision Parts** and Industrial-level requirements.

Community references identify the Watchtower and troop-related buildings among the structures that can enter Industrial progression. Later-state accounts commonly use labels such as Industrial 1, Industrial 3, Industrial 7, and higher.

## Precision Parts

Precision Parts are one of the major late-game progression bottlenecks. They are used for Industrial upgrades and are therefore often saved for event timing rather than spent immediately.

For Server 504 planning, Industrial upgrades should be considered together with:

- Alliance Duel scoring windows;
- Survival Preparedness scoring windows;
- prerequisite building order;
- research and troop unlock requirements;
- available speedups and resources.

## Data-quality note

Old community charts may show outdated caps or old prerequisite values. When the Wiki publishes exact upgrade tables, each table should include:

- data version or verification date;
- whether buffs are excluded;
- prerequisite buildings;
- resource cost;
- base build time;
- Precision Parts cost where applicable.
