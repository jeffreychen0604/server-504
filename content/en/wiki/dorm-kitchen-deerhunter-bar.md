# Dorm, Kitchen & Deerhunter Bar

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Community-backed; exact unlock levels and numeric effects require current UI verification

These three buildings form the shelter's basic survivor-support layer: **housing, food and mood**.

## Dorm

Community references consistently describe the Dorm as increasing survivor population capacity. Public pages disagree on its exact unlock level, so this Wiki does not hard-code the unlock gate yet.

## Kitchen

Early-game/tutorial references show the Kitchen being built to prepare food for the survivor group. A Japanese community reference also describes it as producing survivor food and canned food used for exploration-related activity.

Because public English structure pages currently lack a clean effect table, exact meal production, storage and worker-slot numbers remain provisional.

## Deerhunter Bar

Community references associate the Deerhunter Bar with restoring or increasing survivor mood/happiness. This fits the official game rule that survivor happiness affects productivity, but the exact effect per building level has not been verified from Server 504.

## Dependency relationship

Think of the three as a chain:

`Dorm increases population → Kitchen supports the population → Deerhunter Bar helps sustain happiness/productivity`

Expanding only Dorm capacity without maintaining food and mood support can create population without equivalent shelter efficiency.
