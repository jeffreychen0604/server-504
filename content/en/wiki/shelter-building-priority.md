# Shelter Building Priority & Dependencies

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Framework based on verified building roles + community progression experience

There is no single permanent building order because the correct priority changes with Watchtower prerequisites, event timing, resource bottlenecks and account goals.

## Priority framework

Use five questions in order:

1. **Does the next Watchtower require it?**  
   Hard prerequisites beat optional efficiency.
2. **Does it compound future time?**  
   Library, Construction Hall, Alliance Hall and Research Center can reduce long-term progression friction.
3. **Does it prevent catastrophic loss?**  
   Hospital capacity, Warehouse protection and City Gate defense can matter more before PvP windows.
4. **Does it directly increase the main march?**  
   Military Center and the main faction Camp are high-value combat support.
5. **Can the upgrade be timed with an event?**  
   Long construction is better when it also scores Alliance Duel / Survival Preparedness or uses a temporary construction buff.

## Early shelter

Focus on unlocking the shelter economy and keeping survivor support functional: Dorm, Kitchen, basic resource producers and required Watchtower gates.

## Mid progression

The account increasingly depends on:

- Alliance Hall;
- Research Center and Library;
- Hospital;
- Military Center;
- primary faction Camp;
- Warehouse when large resource stockpiles become exposed.

Recent community progression advice repeatedly recommends keeping Hospital, research, troop training, Military Center and Alliance Hall reasonably close to the Watchtower rather than rushing WT alone.

## Late progression / Industrial

After WT30, the decision changes from only `Do I have Wood/Iron/Electricity?` to `Which scarce material gives the highest permanent value?`

Precision Parts and other specialized materials make optional support-building upgrades a real opportunity cost. Do not spend scarce materials solely for CP if another building unlocks a combat tier, capacity gate or permanent throughput improvement.

## Dependency audit

Public Watchtower prerequisite tables currently conflict at several levels. The Wiki keeps those disputes in [Watchtower Progression Reference](#/wiki/watchtower-progression-reference) rather than copying one table into every building article.

## Event timing

Before committing a multi-day build:

- request/apply relevant construction buffs first;
- activate development-hero bonuses where applicable;
- make sure Alliance Help will be available;
- consider whether completion/upgrade spending can overlap a scoring window.
