# Fighter Heroes

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for listed faction/type pairs except Guy's specialty label; meta ranking intentionally excluded.

Fighter heroes belong to one side of Dark War: Survival's faction triangle. Fighters are strong against Riders and weak against Shooters.

## Combat archetypes

### Tristan

Community data classifies Tristan as Fighter / Combat. His kit follows the standard Active Skill + Normal Attack structure and includes a Fighter-oriented combat passive. He is useful as a straightforward formation combat hero, but exact investment priority depends on the generation of heroes available to the State.

### Francis

Francis is a Fighter / Combat hero whose passive is built around Fighter survivability. Public data describes his kit as increasing Fighter HP, making him structurally different from pure damage-oriented heroes.

### Noah

Noah is a later Fighter / Combat hero in the community database. His passive combines Fighter damage with reduced **normal attack damage taken** by the troop he leads as stars increase. This makes him a good example of why the Wiki should describe a hero's mechanic before assigning a tier.

## Utility archetypes

### Guy

Guy's public data contains a type-label conflict, but his third skill is clearly progression-oriented: increased idle Adventure food gains plus additional energy recovery / free energy at higher stars. This makes him useful beyond direct PvP power.

### Catherine & Rex

Catherine & Rex are listed as a Fighter-faction Radar specialist. Their progression skill increases free Radar task quantity and Radar food yield, making them an account-development hero rather than a pure battlefield specialist.

## Formation note

Faction alignment matters because Fighter heroes can stack with Fighter troop research, faction passives, APC/chip choices and the faction-counter system. Do not interpret this as a rule that every strong Fighter formation must use the same five named heroes; new heroes and Exclusive Equipment can change the optimal lineup.
