# Gathering Ground & Apocalypse Guide

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Gathering Ground, Exploration support and Apocalypse Guide access  
> **Confidence:** HIGH for Apocalypse Guide access; COMMUNITY for exact Exploration scaling

The **Gathering Ground** supports Exploration-related account utility and now also serves as the entry point for the **Apocalypse Guide**.

## Official Apocalypse Guide behavior

Official patch history confirms:

- survivors with **Shelter Level 10 or above** can access the **Apocalypse Guide**;
- the entry is available through the **Gathering Ground** building;
- the Guide shows information on upcoming major events so players can prepare in advance.

This turns the Gathering Ground into more than a passive stat building: it is also a planning interface for future event timing.

## Community-backed Exploration effects

Older Fandom building data shows Gathering Ground levels affecting:

- **Daily Exploration Attempts**;
- **Rare Task Refresh Rate**.

The exact per-level values are not locked here because the public table is incomplete and may be stale.

## Practical use

Check the Gathering Ground when planning a week, not only when an Exploration task appears.

Useful questions:

- What major event is coming next?
- Should resources/speedups/tickets be saved?
- Are Exploration attempts close to cap?
- Is the current task pool worth refreshing or completing now?

## Do not confuse with Mart

- **Mart** → world-map resource gathering efficiency.
- **Gathering Ground** → Exploration-related utility and Apocalypse Guide access.
