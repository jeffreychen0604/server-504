# President & Presidential Palace Officials

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for current Officials UI changes and term rewards; broad President powers are community-documented and should be verified in the live Palace interface.

The President is a State-level role tied to Capital control and the **Presidential Palace** administration interface.

## President selection context

Community Capital Clash documentation describes the controlling alliance as being able to designate the State President after the Capital is secured. Cross-state State Ruler / State of Supremacy variants can add additional authority or Supreme President rules.

Those appointment conditions are event-sensitive, so the active event Rules page remains authoritative.

## Official positions currently referenced by patch notes

An April 2026 official patch explicitly names the following Presidential Palace official positions:

- **Grand Marshal**;
- **Supreme General**;
- **Secretary of Security**;
- **Secretary of Agriculture**;
- **Secretary of Science**;
- **Secretary of Construction**.

The patch added self-resignation for those positions after serving for more than **30 minutes**.

This confirms the positions and Officials system without requiring the Wiki to infer their exact buffs.

## President term recognition

A later official update states that, starting from the next term, becoming President grants a **term-exclusive avatar frame and title**.

## Community-documented President functions

Capital Clash community references describe the President as able to:

- appoint officials;
- grant State gifts/rewards;
- change the State declaration/name-related presentation;
- activate or distribute State-level administrative benefits.

These broad functions are useful orientation, but exact button names, cooldowns, buff values and gift limits should be captured from the current Presidential Palace UI.

## Governance boundary for Server 504

Holding the in-game President role does **not automatically define Server 504 governance authority** on this website.

The game role provides game mechanics. Server-wide agreements, alliance representation, voting thresholds and Capital rotation are governed separately by the Server 504 Charter and Operational Codex.
