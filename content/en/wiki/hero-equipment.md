# Hero Equipment

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for Power Core usage and equipment progression concepts; medium for exact stat values and shop availability.

Hero equipment is a major combat-power layer in Dark War: Survival and should be developed together with the hero and formation rather than treated as cosmetic gear.

## Standard equipment

Standard hero equipment provides combat attributes such as attack, defense, HP and troop-related bonuses. Equipment quality progresses through multiple rarity tiers, with **Orange** equipment serving as the key long-term baseline in older and current community guides.

A practical development rule is to prioritize the main formation before spreading high-quality gear across many secondary heroes.

## Enhancement vs. upgrade

Two progression ideas should be kept separate:

- **Enhancement** raises equipment performance using enhancement materials or consumed equipment.
- **Upgrade / star progression** uses rarer materials and becomes available on high-quality equipment after reaching the required enhancement level.

The in-game description of **Power Core** explicitly states that it is used to upgrade hero equipment and that Orange equipment can be upgraded after it has been enhanced to **Lv.20**.

## Power Cores

Power Cores are a scarce progression material. Community data lists recurring sources such as:

- Season Shop
- VIP Shop
- legacy Capital / Arena / Black Gold shop families now represented by the current Honor system where applicable
- Trial Shop
- selected event rewards
- Bio-Mutant damage rewards
- paid offers

Shop contents and event thresholds can change, so the Wiki should verify current availability before publishing exact purchase priorities.

## Exclusive Equipment

Exclusive Equipment is separate from standard equipment and is tied to specific heroes. It can provide:

- additional hero-specific skills;
- troop or faction attributes;
- progression through its own star/breakthrough system.

Official 2026 patch notes also confirm advanced Exclusive Equipment systems such as Red Star breakthrough and star-level swapping using a dedicated item.

## Practical priorities

For most developing accounts:

1. fully equip the main formation before secondary formations;
2. use front-line gear to improve survivability and back-line gear to improve damage output;
3. avoid spending Power Cores without an equipment plan;
4. time expensive upgrades with scoring events when possible;
5. verify whether a hero's Exclusive Equipment materially changes the formation before investing heavily.
