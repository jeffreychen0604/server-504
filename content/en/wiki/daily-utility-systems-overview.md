# Daily Utility Systems — Overview

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Recruitment, Black Market, gathering, Exploration, Energy and daily utility loops  
> **Confidence:** Mixed — official current behavior where available; older building values remain community reference only

Dark War: Survival has several systems that are not major combat modes by themselves but quietly determine long-term account efficiency. These are the systems worth checking on a routine rather than only when an event forces you to use them.

## Core daily utility loop

| System | Main purpose | Current confidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Radio Station | Hero recruitment and free daily recruitment | HIGH for free daily recruit; hero pools remain version-sensitive |
| Black Market | Discounted resources, speedups, tickets and other rotating items | HIGH for refresh behavior |
| Mart | World-map gathering speed and load | COMMUNITY — exact level values need current capture |
| Gathering Ground | Exploration support and Apocalypse Guide access | HIGH for Apocalypse Guide; numeric Exploration values need capture |
| Adventure Camp | Exploration/adventure management and idle/adventure reward flow | COMMUNITY |
| Supply Station | Energy recovery/claim utility | HIGH for current direct-claim behavior; refresh quantities need capture |
| Radar | Radar events, scouting utility and Quick Start | HIGH for Quick Start Lv.15 |

## Why these systems matter

The mistake is treating every red dot as equally important. A better model is to protect the actions that create recurring free value:

1. claim time-gated free recruitment;
2. inspect discounted Black Market inventory before spending Rubies elsewhere;
3. prevent Exploration/idle rewards from sitting at cap;
4. claim Energy before a world-activity session;
5. keep gathering infrastructure relevant when RSS becomes the account bottleneck;
6. use Radar Quick Start and routine clearing to reduce manual friction.

## Stable rules vs version-sensitive numbers

This Wiki treats **feature identity** separately from **exact values**.

For example:

- official App Store editorial confirms a daily free recruit and daily Black Market refresh;
- official patch notes confirm Radar Quick Start at Radar Lv.15;
- community pages provide Mart percentages and Gathering Ground Exploration values, but those tables are not safe to treat as permanent current data without Server 504 screenshots.

That distinction prevents a useful guide from becoming a stale spreadsheet.
