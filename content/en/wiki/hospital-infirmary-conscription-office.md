# Hospital, Infirmary & Conscription Office

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Hospital/State modifiers official+community; Infirmary and Conscription details community-backed

These systems all deal with recovery, but they should not be treated as interchangeable.

## Hospital

The Hospital is the primary wounded-unit capacity system. Community structure pages describe upgrades as increasing healing capacity. Official 2026 Origin Lands patch notes also apply temporary modifiers directly to **Hospital Capacity**, confirming that Hospital capacity remains an active combat-risk variable.

During certain Origin Lands War Days, official patch notes granted temporary bonuses including higher healing speed, lower healing cost and **+10,000 Hospital Capacity**. Those are event modifiers, not permanent base values.

## Infirmary

Public sources conflict here:

- some community references describe the Infirmary as treating **sick survivors**;
- an older archived page describes it as additional wounded-unit healing capacity.

Because the terminology is inconsistent, the Wiki does not merge Infirmary into Hospital. Current Server 504 UI should decide the canonical function.

## Conscription Office

Community documentation describes the Conscription Office as a reserve-recovery layer used when wounded exceed Hospital capacity. It is said to consume **Reserve Army Badges** and to scale its reserve capacity from Hospital capacity rather than being normally upgraded itself.

This is strategically different from healing: it is a loss-mitigation/replacement system after capacity overflow.

## Practical risk model

`incoming wounded → Hospital capacity → overflow risk → reserve/recovery systems → permanent loss if unrecovered`

Before heavy PvP, verify capacity rather than judging safety only by troop count.
