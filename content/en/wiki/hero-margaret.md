# Margaret

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Shooter  
> **Specialty:** Shooter HP / Shooter battle damage

Margaret is a Shooter combat hero built around **durability plus battle-damage scaling**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Margaret from the **Goodie Bazaar** event.

## Skill structure

### Inferno Core — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero scaling pattern.

### Inferno Gun — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Eye of Precision — Passive Skill

Public data describes Margaret as increasing Shooters' HP, while star progression adds **Shooter battle damage**.

That combination gives her a straightforward role: help the Shooter march survive while continuing to improve faction damage output.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Main/secondary Shooter combat / depends on star depth.**

Margaret is most useful when:

- Shooter is the account's main faction;
- the formation needs more HP without giving up damage scaling;
- she is already materially starred;
- a second Shooter formation needs a durable combat core.

She has no separate progression-economy utility, so replacement should be evaluated mainly through formation performance and rebuild cost.

## Exclusive Equipment

As an Orange hero, Margaret is eligible for the game's Exclusive Equipment system once the relevant State and star requirements are met. Current hero-specific equipment effects still require in-game verification.
