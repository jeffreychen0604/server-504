# Noah

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Season:** 4  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Fighter  
> **Specialty:** Fighter damage / normal-attack damage mitigation

Noah is a Fighter combat hero whose kit combines **faction damage output** with damage-taken reduction against normal attacks.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Noah as a **Legend Battle Pass** hero.

## Skill structure

### Quake Hammer — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero damage pattern.

### Force Hammer — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Stand of Protection — Passive Skill

Public data describes two important layers:

- a baseline increase to Fighters' combat damage;
- progressive reduction to **normal attack damage taken** by Noah's troop.

That combination gives Noah a straightforward identity: improve Fighter output while helping the march absorb repeated normal attacks.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Main-faction combat / high if already starred.**

Noah is most relevant when:

- Fighter is the primary faction;
- the account values durability without giving up offensive faction scaling;
- his stars are already materially ahead of alternatives;
- the player needs a robust Fighter march for sustained PvP or objective combat.

His acquisition path can make replacement expensive: a newer hero is not automatically better if replacing Noah would require rebuilding stars, equipment and formation synergy from scratch.

## Exclusive Equipment

As an Orange hero, Noah is eligible for the game's Exclusive Equipment system once the State and star requirements are met. Hero-specific effects still require current in-game capture before publication.
