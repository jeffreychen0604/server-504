# Factions & Heroes

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for faction structure; medium for hero-specific values because balance data can change.

Heroes and troops in Dark War: Survival are organized around three combat factions:

- **Fighter**
- **Rider**
- **Shooter**

Community reference data consistently describes a counter triangle:

| Faction | Strong against | Weak against |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fighter | Rider | Shooter |
| Rider | Shooter | Fighter |
| Shooter | Fighter | Rider |

Same-faction formations are important because faction bonuses, research, chips and hero passives can multiply the same troop type. For most players, concentrating investment into one primary faction produces a stronger main formation than spreading early resources evenly across all three.

## Hero progression

A hero's practical strength comes from several layers:

1. **Level** — raises base hero attributes.
2. **Stars / fragments** — improves skills and progression bonuses.
3. **Skills** — active, normal attack and progression-oriented skills vary by hero.
4. **Standard equipment** — provides combat attributes and can be enhanced/upgraded.
5. **Exclusive Equipment** — hero-specific equipment that can add unique skills and additional attributes.
6. **Formation context** — faction match, troop type, front/back position and the rest of the team matter as much as isolated hero CP.

## Formation principle

For a developing account, a useful default is:

- choose one primary faction;
- focus the strongest five heroes that support that formation;
- align troop research and APC/chip investment with that faction;
- build a second formation only after the main one has a solid equipment and research base.

This is a strategy guideline rather than a fixed game rule. Specific heroes can justify mixed lineups when their skills are strong enough.

## Hero data policy

Hero-specific skill numbers are especially vulnerable to balance changes. The Server 504 Wiki should therefore store hero pages with:

- hero name;
- rarity/class;
- faction;
- role/specialty;
- acquisition source;
- active and passive skill descriptions;
- exclusive equipment, if available;
- **last verified date**;
- source screenshot or current data reference when possible.

Avoid copying an old tier list as permanent truth. Tier strength changes with new heroes, Exclusive Equipment, Industrial progression, chips and seasonal mechanics.
