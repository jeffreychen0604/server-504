# City Gate, Durability & Shelter Attacks

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for current Defense Preset and official durability changes; exact shelter durability values require in-game verification.

Shelter defense has two different failure layers: **the fighting formation** and **shelter durability**. Winning or losing troop combat is not the same thing as burning down a shelter's wall.

## City Gate defense

Current official patches introduced **Defense Preset** on the City Gate screen.

Survivors can pre-set multiple defense squads. If heroes in the highest-priority preset are busy elsewhere, the next preset can be used automatically. If no secondary preset exists, the game can auto-deploy the strongest available heroes.

A later official patch added another restriction: when multiple defense squads are configured, **one hero can only belong to one defense squad at a time**.

## Durability and Destruction Value

Official October 2025 balance notes substantially changed world-building destruction:

- Watchtower durability restoration cooldown: **5 min → 60 min**;
- solo attack Basic Destruction Value: **3,000 → 6,000**;
- rally attack Basic Destruction Value: **3,000 → 18,000**.

At the same time, certain event-specific destruction bonuses were reduced to offset the stronger base values.

Those numbers are important historical/current reference points, but shelter, Watchtower, city and season-object durability are separate systems and should not be merged into one generic formula.

## Shelter burning

Official patches explicitly improved burning visual effects as City Wall durability decreases. That confirms durability loss is a persistent map-state mechanic independent from the immediate battle report.

## Special-zone danger

Shelters inside Trade Post / Merchant Guild dark zones can receive much higher durability pressure than ordinary map positions. See the dedicated dark-zone article before evaluating risk based only on troop strength.

## Operational checklist before war

- configure at least one valid Defense Preset;
- ensure critical heroes are not all locked in incompatible activities;
- check Hospital capacity;
- know whether the shelter is in a special combat zone;
- distinguish troop losses from durability damage in reports and map state.
