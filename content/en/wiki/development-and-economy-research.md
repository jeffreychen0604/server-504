# Development & Economy Research

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Community-backed structure; exact node values pending current UI capture

Development and Economy research improve the account's underlying efficiency rather than directly winning one battle. Their value compounds because construction, research, training, gathering and healing happen continuously.

## Development research

Current community guides place systems such as the following under the broader Development path:

- formation / march availability;
- construction efficiency;
- research efficiency;
- training-related capacity or speed;
- hospital-related capacity or recovery efficiency;
- other shelter-wide throughput improvements.

One frequently cited recommendation is to unlock additional **Formations** early because more simultaneous marches improve gathering, rallies and map activity. The exact node name, level requirement and costs should be checked in the current Research Center UI before being entered as canonical.

## Economy research

Economy research generally focuses on:

- gathering speed;
- resource-production efficiency;
- resource-specific gathering or production bonuses;
- long-term farming throughput.

Economy nodes often look less impressive than direct combat CP, but they improve the rate at which future buildings, technologies and troop training can be funded.

## Development vs Economy: which first?

A practical priority rule is:

1. remove a hard bottleneck first — formation count, research throughput, training capacity, hospital capacity;
2. take high-frequency efficiency bonuses next — research/construction/gathering speed;
3. fill resource-specific economy nodes when they solve a real bottleneck;
4. do not delay essential Duel or Battle Strategy milestones only to complete low-impact economy nodes.

## Event timing

Research completion can overlap with recurring event scoring. Survival Preparedness has historically included:

- Tech CP gained;
- Wisdom Medals consumed;
- Research Speedups consumed.

Exact point values can change and should be checked on the active event page. The permanent rule is simpler: if an upgrade is not urgently needed, completing or claiming it during a matching scoring window can create extra value from the same research investment.

## Industrial-stage note

Industrial accounts often stop being limited purely by Wood/Iron/Electricity and become constrained by specialized materials, long timers and late research requirements. That changes priority from "finish the cheapest node" to "protect scarce resources for the branch that unlocks the next meaningful account milestone."

The Wiki does not yet have a verified complete Industrial Research tree. Exact late-game node costs remain in the verification queue.

## Avoid these mistakes

- researching low-impact economy nodes while a major formation/queue bottleneck is still open;
- spending all Research Speedups outside scoring windows for non-urgent nodes;
- assuming older tech screenshots still match the current tree;
- treating raw Research CP as more important than the effect unlocked.
