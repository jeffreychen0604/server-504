# Progression Planning

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for planning principles; exact costs and unlocks remain versioned data.

Efficient progression in Dark War: Survival is less about upgrading everything and more about keeping the next progression gate ready before the current one finishes.

## Core planning loop

A practical progression cycle is:

1. identify the next Watchtower requirement;
2. pre-build its prerequisite while the current Watchtower upgrade is running;
3. keep the main faction camp and Battle research close enough to use the next troop milestone;
4. bank resources and speedups for the intended event window;
5. after WT30, replace normal-resource planning with a Precision Part / Industrial prerequisite plan.

## Early and mid-game bottlenecks

### Alliance Hall

Alliance Hall appears repeatedly in higher Watchtower prerequisite chains and is one of the most common reasons a player reaches the next WT level but cannot start the following upgrade.

### Research Center

Research Center acts as another periodic gate and should not be allowed to fall far behind. Even when it is not the immediate Watchtower prerequisite, troop research can still block the practical benefit of a new camp level.

### Resource transition

Community progression tables agree that the resource burden broadens in the mid-game and rises sharply in the WT20+ range. Players approaching this point should stop thinking only in terms of the current upgrade and instead budget for a multi-level push.

## Event timing

Construction resources have two values:

- the permanent power/progression they buy;
- the event points they can generate when spent at the right time.

For Server 504 players, large Watchtower, Industrial and prerequisite upgrades should be checked against the current Alliance Duel and Survival Preparedness windows before using speedups or scarce materials.

This does **not** mean every upgrade should be delayed. If delaying a progression gate costs days of troop/research growth, the lost account growth can be more expensive than the event points gained.

## Industrial planning

Once Industrial progression begins:

- Precision Parts become a hard planning resource;
- one displayed Industrial level may require multiple sub-upgrades;
- faction camps can compete with the Watchtower for the same scarce material;
- broad upgrades are often weaker than completing the minimum prerequisite path for the next meaningful power milestone.

A useful rule is to choose a **primary combat faction** before making expensive Industrial camp investments.

## What to record before an expensive push

Before a major Watchtower or Industrial upgrade, record:

- current Watchtower / Industrial level;
- exact prerequisite buildings;
- Precision Parts required;
- raw resources required;
- base build time shown;
- active construction-cost reduction heroes or buffs;
- current event scoring window;
- expected troop or research unlock after completion.

These screenshots can also feed back into the Server 504 Wiki so later players receive current-state data rather than old community estimates.

## Data policy

The Wiki intentionally separates **planning principles** from **hard-number tables**. Planning principles remain useful across patches. Numeric tables must carry a verification date and should be replaced when Server 504 in-game evidence contradicts them.
