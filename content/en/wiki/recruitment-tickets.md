# Recruitment Tickets

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Recruitment-ticket classes, sources and spend discipline  
> **Confidence:** COMMUNITY for source lists; current Radio Station UI wins for hero pools

Recruitment tickets are the consumable layer behind the Radio Station. Their value depends less on the ticket color/name itself than on **which recruitment pool it opens** and **which heroes are currently available in that pool**.

## Standard Recruitment Tickets

DarkWarData documents standard Recruitment Tickets as usable at the Radio Station, with historical sources including:

- Adventure Battles;
- mail rewards;
- Allied Power;
- Ruby Shop;
- premium/battle-pass style rewards;
- special offers.

Those source lists are useful for orientation but should not be treated as a permanent current loot table.

## Elite Recruitment Tickets

Community data documents Elite Recruitment Tickets as a rarer ticket type used for Elite Recruitment. Historical sources include Season Shop, Season End rewards and special offers.

The exact Elite hero roster has changed over time. Do not use an old list to decide whether to spend tickets today — open the current Radio Station pool preview first.

## Prime / other recruitment tickets

DarkWarData hero profiles for Corleone and Megan document **Prime Recruitment Tickets** as a Radio Station acquisition path. Because naming and pool availability can depend on progression/state generation, the Wiki records the ticket family but does not invent a universal acquisition table.

## Spend framework

Use this order:

1. confirm the current hero pool;
2. identify whether a target hero materially improves your main formation or progression economy;
3. check whether an active event rewards recruitment;
4. avoid spending rare tickets solely to clear a red dot;
5. preserve enough tickets for future pool rotations if no current target is high value.
