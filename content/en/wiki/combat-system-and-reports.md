# Combat System & Battle Reports

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current world-battle/report behavior; medium for community-tested damage-engine details.

Dark War: Survival uses the same account investments across several battle modes, but the way those investments are expressed is not identical in every fight. For practical planning, separate **troop battles** from **hero-battle / Adventure-style combat**.

## Troop battles

Troop battles are the main reference for server warfare. They include world-map PvP, shelter attacks and defenses, rallies, many alliance fights and other engagements that generate a battle report.

A battle report is more useful than the win/loss banner because it can expose the actual sources behind a formation's strength, including:

- troop count and remaining troops;
- hero contribution;
- Hero Equipment;
- technologies;
- Modified Vehicle / APC bonuses;
- faction-related bonuses;
- combat rounds and skill activations.

Community battle-log testing shows that troop combat combines many percentage bonuses into a shared formation-level combat pool. This is why a lower displayed CP formation can sometimes perform better when its faction research, hero passives and vehicle bonuses are better aligned.

## Hero battles / Adventure combat

Adventure-style encounters visibly resolve around individual heroes. Community testing distinguishes front-line defensive roles from back-line damage roles and reports that individual hero stats and gear allocation matter more directly here than in troop battles.

The precise hidden formulas are not officially published, so Server 504 Wiki should treat exact damage formulas as **community-tested**, not official constants.

## How to use reports for improvement

When reviewing a loss, check the gap in this order:

1. **Troop tier and troop count** — an undersized formation loses output and durability quickly.
2. **Faction matchup** — Fighter, Rider and Shooter counters can change an otherwise close fight.
3. **Hero passives / formation buffs** — these often scale the whole troop rather than one hero.
4. **Research** — especially primary-faction ATK/DEF/HP/damage research.
5. **Hero Equipment** — compare the actual report contribution, not only equipment CP.
6. **Modified Vehicle and chips** — late-game vehicle bonuses can be one of the largest report lines.
7. **Season, alliance, wonder, skin and other account-wide bonuses**.

## Damage-cycle caution

Several community test reports describe a repeating normal-attack / skill-activation cycle and show skills firing at predictable round intervals. Because the developer does not publish the complete combat formula, the Wiki should not present reverse-engineered round formulas as guaranteed behavior without a current Server 504 report to verify them.

## Server 504 evidence standard

For future combat-formula articles, preserve screenshots of:

- the troop overview;
- Troop Buffs pages;
- Hero Equipment comparison;
- round-by-round log;
- attacker and defender formation composition.

This allows formulas to be re-tested after patches instead of relying on old guides.
