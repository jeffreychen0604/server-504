# Corleone

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Tech Research

Corleone is a Rider hero whose defining value is **research progression**.

## Acquisition

Community data lists:

- **Prime Recruitment Tickets** in the Radio Station;
- **Goodie Bazaar**.

## Skill structure

### Rain Fire — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill.

### Desert Eagle — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Scientist — Progression Skill

The defining skill for Corleone. Public data lists:

- additional free research time;
- reduced basic resource cost for research;
- increasing research speed through star progression;
- larger free-research windows at higher star breakpoints.

Because research is a permanent account layer, Corleone can remain valuable even after he leaves a main combat formation.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Progression utility / keep invested.**

Best-fit use cases:

- large research queues;
- event timing for Tech Research scoring;
- reducing long-cycle research burden;
- Rider accounts that can also make use of his faction alignment.

Do not compare Corleone to pure combat Riders only by march damage. His research economy is a separate value stream.
