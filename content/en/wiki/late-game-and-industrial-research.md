# Late-Game & Industrial Research

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Framework-level only; exact late-game tree requires Server 504 capture

After Watchtower 30 and the transition into Industrial progression, research planning becomes less about clearing cheap early nodes and more about managing specialized bottlenecks, long timers and combat breakpoints.

## What changes in late game

Early research is often limited by ordinary resources and short prerequisites. Late-game accounts increasingly face:

- longer research timers;
- larger Gold/resource requirements;
- scarce Wisdom Medal decisions;
- Industrial building/material bottlenecks;
- faction specialization;
- event/season-conditional technologies;
- higher opportunity cost for every queue slot.

## Research and Industrial progression are connected

Industrial progression already competes for resources such as Precision Parts and other specialized materials. Even when a research node does not directly consume those materials, research timing still competes with construction for:

- speedups;
- ordinary resources;
- event windows;
- player attention and queue availability.

This is why late-game planning should use a combined construction/research roadmap rather than two independent lists.

## Late-game priority model

Before starting a long technology, ask:

1. Does it unlock a required building/troop/research milestone?
2. Does it materially improve the main war formation?
3. Is it prerequisite tax for a much stronger node?
4. Does it consume Wisdom Medals that are needed for Duel/Battle Strategy elsewhere?
5. Can the completion overlap with Alliance Duel or Survival Preparedness?
6. Will using speedups here delay a higher-value Industrial construction push?

## Do not publish inferred cost tables

Public guides often discuss late-game research broadly, but current full Industrial/advanced tree data is incomplete. The Wiki therefore does not manufacture a cost table from account screenshots of unknown server age.

## Current official example of late research being season-aware

Official 2026 patch history states that `Research Center → Battle Strategy → Kill Training` technologies apply on Wednesday and Saturday War Days during Origin Lands.

That is a strong example of why late-game technology must be documented with **scope and season**, not just effect value.
