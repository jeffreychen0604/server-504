# Alliance Help & Alliance Hall

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for the core mechanic; exact per-level values remain community data until captured in game.

Alliance Help is one of the highest-value free progression systems in Dark War: Survival. Alliance members can reduce one another's **construction** and **research** timers by tapping Help requests.

## Alliance Hall

The personal **Alliance Hall** governs two important capacities:

1. **Alliance Help capacity** — how many helps an upgrade can receive;
2. **reinforcement capacity** — how many allied units can reinforce your shelter.

Community Wiki data explicitly states that higher Alliance Hall levels increase reinforcement capacity and alliance-help chances.

## Progression importance

Alliance Hall is also a recurring Watchtower prerequisite. Public progression tables show it becoming a major gate from roughly WT9 onward. Because Watchtower prerequisite tables conflict at some levels, the exact chain remains versioned in the separate Watchtower reference, but the planning rule is stable:

> Do not let Alliance Hall fall far behind the Watchtower.

## Practical use

- Request Help before spending construction/research speedups.
- Keep Alliance Hall progressing during long Watchtower upgrades.
- In active alliances, Help compounds across dozens of upgrades and saves a significant amount of speedup inventory over time.
- Reinforcement capacity matters separately from rally capacity; do not treat the two as the same stat.
