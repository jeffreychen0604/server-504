# Exclusive Equipment & Hall of Honor

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current Red Star / Swap / Hall of Honor behavior confirmed by official patch notes; medium for hero-specific effects and fragment costs.

**Exclusive Equipment** is hero-specific progression that exists separately from standard Hero Equipment. It can add unique hero effects and additional troop or faction attributes, so unlocking it can materially change a hero's value.

## What is confirmed

Official patches confirm that Exclusive Equipment has progressed beyond its original star system and now includes:

- unlocking and upgrading hero-specific Exclusive Equipment;
- **breakthrough** progression to **Red Star**;
- **Hall of Honor** progress associated with the equipment;
- **Exclusive Equipment Swap** between eligible Red Star items;
- the dedicated **Core of Rotation** item for a swap.

When a swap succeeds, the two eligible Exclusive Equipment pieces exchange star levels and their Hall of Honor progress is swapped with them.

## Core of Rotation

Official February 2026 notes state that each Exclusive Equipment Swap consumes **1 Core of Rotation**.

Because this is a specialized respec-like resource, it should not be treated as an ordinary upgrade material.

## Acquisition evolves over time

Community guides from the feature's original release describe a much narrower set of Exclusive Equipment sources. Later official patches added Exclusive Equipment Fragments to the Daily Sale and introduced systems such as the Flea Market that can exchange surplus hero fragments for Exclusive Equipment fragments and other rare items.

Therefore, old statements such as “this equipment is only obtainable from one paid pack” should not be copied into the Wiki without a date.

## Investment rule

Before investing heavily, check:

1. Is the hero part of the current main formation?
2. Does the Exclusive Equipment add a formation-wide effect or mainly personal damage?
3. Is a newer-season hero likely to replace this hero soon?
4. Does the next breakthrough unlock a meaningful effect?
5. Would preserving fragments or a Core of Rotation offer more flexibility?

## What not to hard-code yet

Fragment cost per star, breakthrough cost and hero-specific Exclusive Equipment effects should be stored per hero and verified against Server 504's current interface. These values are more likely to change than the general system structure.
