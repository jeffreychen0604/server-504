# Research Queues, Speed & Timing

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Strong for timing principles; exact VIP/queue unlock values require current UI verification

Research progression is not only about choosing the right technology. It is also about keeping the Research Center active and consuming speedups at the right time.

## Research Speed

The Research Center building itself increases Research Speed as it levels according to community structure data. Additional research-speed effects can also come from account progression, heroes, VIP or technologies depending the current version.

Exact stacking formulas are not yet published as canonical on this Wiki.

## Research queues

Community guides report access to an additional research queue through VIP progression and temporary VIP activation strategies. Because exact VIP unlock wording can change, the Wiki does not currently hard-code a specific VIP level as an official rule without a fresh in-game capture.

The practical rule remains:

**an idle research queue is lost progression time.**

If a second queue is available, use it to separate:

- one long-duration strategic research;
- one shorter branch-clearing or prerequisite research.

## Research Speedups

Research Speedups are most efficient when they solve one of three problems:

1. reach a meaningful technology breakpoint;
2. free a queue that would otherwise sit blocked;
3. overlap progression with a scoring event.

Using large speedup reserves merely to finish a low-impact node immediately is usually poor timing.

## Claim timing

When an event scores **Tech CP gained**, the exact moment at which progress is recognized matters. Depending on event implementation, completing or claiming an upgrade inside the scoring window can matter more than when the research was started.

Before staging long research across reset, verify the active event Rules page rather than assuming the scoring trigger.

## Double-dip logic

The highest-value research window is one where several scoring actions overlap, for example:

- Tech CP gained;
- Research Speedups consumed;
- Wisdom Medals consumed.

This is the same principle Server 504 already applies to other save-and-spend systems: preserve non-urgent resources until one action satisfies multiple event objectives.

## What should never wait

Do not delay research purely for event points when the delay blocks:

- a Watchtower prerequisite;
- a troop-tier unlock;
- a key Formation/queue upgrade;
- a major Duel Research reward breakpoint;
- an essential Battle Strategy technology before war.

Permanent progression lost for several days can be worth more than the extra event points.
