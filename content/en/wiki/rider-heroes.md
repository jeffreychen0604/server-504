# Rider Heroes

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for faction/type/acquisition in the listed community database; current meta ranking intentionally excluded.

Rider heroes sit between Fighters and Shooters in the faction counter triangle. Riders are strong against Shooters and weak against Fighters.

## Progression specialists

### Quinn

Quinn is a Rider hero whose third skill is an **Alliance Duel / unit training progression skill** rather than a pure faction combat passive. Community data lists resource reduction for training at base level and, at higher stars, increasing training speed and Alliance Duel points. This makes Quinn valuable to an account even when another Rider might be stronger in direct combat.

### Corleone

Corleone is a Rider / Tech Research specialist. His progression skill reduces research burden by increasing free research time and research speed as stars increase. This is long-cycle account value rather than just formation CP.

## Combat specialists

### Cyrus

Cyrus is a Rider / Combat hero with a Rider-oriented passive. His public skill data follows the typical S-rarity combat structure: Active Skill, Normal Attack, faction passive and Exclusive Talent.

### Marcia

Marcia is a Rider / Combat hero whose public passive data focuses on Rider HP and additional Rider battle damage at higher stars.

### Katrina

Katrina is a Rider / Combat hero whose passive progression increases troop normal-attack damage. Community data consistently spells the name **Katrina**.

An official 2025 patch note uses **Katerina** while mentioning an Exclusive Equipment animation optimization. The Wiki therefore keeps `Katrina` as the working community name but flags the spelling for Server 504 UI verification.

### Lucas

Lucas is a later Rider / Combat hero in the public database. His passive combines Rider damage with reduced counterattack damage taken by the troop he leads, giving him a different defensive profile from Marcia or Katrina.

## Investment note

Do not read roster order as a tier list. Later-generation combat heroes may outperform earlier ones at equal investment, while already-developed heroes can remain more efficient than replacing them. Exclusive Equipment is another major variable.
