# APC & Modified Vehicle

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current feature names and 2025 official patch changes; medium for older unlock-level tables.

The **APC / Modified Vehicle** system supports world-map formations and becomes a major source of combat power as an account matures.

## Core progression layers

The system has evolved over time and now contains several separate layers:

- vehicle / modification level;
- Modified Vehicle parts;
- vehicle chips;
- part set bonuses;
- Tactical Modification;
- vehicle skins and related bonuses.

Older community references describe an earlier APC progression model with lower caps. These numbers should not be treated as current because official patch notes later expanded the system substantially.

## Important official changes

Official October 2025 patch notes documented major expansion of Modified Vehicle progression:

- the maximum **Modify** level was raised to **500**;
- upgrading six parts to specified levels can activate **Parts Set** bonuses and unlock skins;
- part-upgrade resource requirements were changed;
- vehicle chip progression became part of advanced unlock conditions;
- **Tactical Modification** was introduced as an endgame layer.

Tactical Modification can unlock when any one of several advanced conditions is met, including a high vehicle transformation level, sufficiently developed parts, or a highly starred Orange vehicle chip.

## Chips and formations

Vehicle chips should be aligned with formation composition. Official patch notes confirm that the game can recommend troop setups by CP or faction and can intelligently match chip setups for defensive formations based on troop numbers and unit types.

This reinforces a broader development rule: APC/chip investment should support the same primary faction being strengthened through troop research and hero development.

## Chip Factory

The game includes chip crafting/progression systems, but exact access requirements and recipes have changed over time. Until a current in-game capture is available, the Server 504 Wiki should avoid publishing old crafting costs as fixed truth.

Recommended article fields for future Chip Factory data:

- unlock requirement;
- material inputs;
- rarity/output pool;
- crafting time or limits;
- star-up requirements;
- recycle/exchange behavior;
- event interactions.

## Data-quality warning

A community APC table published in early 2025 listed much lower maximum part/vehicle levels. Official later patches clearly superseded that information. This is a good example of why the Server 504 Wiki should always prefer official dated patch notes when system caps conflict with older guides.
