# Bio-Mutant — Frankenstein

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current round rules and timing changes; medium for damage thresholds/reward tables because alliance tiers and patches can change them.

**Bio-Mutant**, commonly referred to as the **Frankenstein** event, is an alliance damage event. R4/R5 leadership schedules or starts the target, alliance members rally it, and rewards are based on personal and alliance damage.

## Core event flow

Community event data describes the event as:

1. R4/R5 starts or schedules Frankenstein.
2. The target appears near the summoner's shelter.
3. Alliance members rally the target during the active window.
4. Attacks do not consume normal energy for the event target.
5. Rewards are settled from personal damage and total alliance damage.

The exact reward thresholds shown in old charts should not be treated as permanent.

## Two-round system

Official January 2026 patch notes added a major rule for developed alliances:

- once **Alliance Gifts reach Lv.20**, two Frankenstein rounds can be activated in one Bio-Mutant event;
- round 2 must begin at least **10 minutes after round 1 ends**;
- each survivor may participate in only **one round per event**;
- each survivor can receive rewards only once per event;
- if a survivor registers for both rounds, reward settlement occurs after round 2 concludes.

This creates a leadership decision: split members across two rounds to maximize participation and total alliance output instead of stacking every strong player into the same round.

## Placement interval

Official January 2026 notes also clarified that, except for the initial placement, there is a **10-minute interval** between Frankenstein placements.

## Damage optimization

Community event guidance consistently recommends:

- join the strongest efficient rally available;
- use the highest-tier troops your account can sustain;
- align hero faction, troop faction and APC/chip setup;
- ensure the rally leader has strong rally capacity and combat stats;
- avoid wasting a limited participation opportunity on a poor preset;
- verify the formation before the event begins rather than rebuilding during the active window.

## Leadership coordination

For one-round events, leadership should prioritize a start time with the highest possible attendance.

For two-round events, leadership should decide before the first summon:

- which players are assigned to Round 1;
- which players are assigned to Round 2;
- whether major rally leads should be divided between rounds;
- how to handle members who can attend only one time slot;
- when each Frankenstein will be placed.

Because each survivor only receives one participation/reward opportunity, the two-round feature is primarily an attendance and alliance-damage optimization tool.

## Rewards

Community data identifies Bio-Mutant as a source of valuable growth materials, including **Power Cores** at sufficiently high personal damage milestones and equipment-enhancement materials such as **Boost Ores**.

Exact damage thresholds and reward quantities are version-sensitive and should be captured from the current Server 504 event interface before being published as a numeric table.

## What to prepare

Before the event:

- heal troops;
- load the correct combat preset;
- check hero equipment and Exclusive Equipment;
- check APC/chips;
- avoid sending your strongest march away on a long task near start time;
- confirm which round you are assigned to if the alliance uses two rounds.
