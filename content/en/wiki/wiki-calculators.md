# Resource Calculators

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Planning tools only. Each calculator shows whether its formula is official or community-observed.

Use these calculators for quick planning without treating uncertain community values as permanent game rules.

## Calculator confidence

### Honor migration conversion — OFFICIAL

When Capital Shop, Black Gold Shop and Arena Shop were merged into Honor Shop, the official conversion was:

- **1 Capital Glory Badge = 40 Honor Points**
- **1 Black Gold Coin = 2 Honor Points**

This is a historical conversion rule from the shop migration, not a formula for current event earnings.

### Orange Chip planning — COMMUNITY OBSERVED

Community reports repeatedly cite **800 Advanced Chip Materials** to craft one selected Orange Chip in Chip Factory. Public official patch notes confirm the Chip System and Chip Material sources, but do not publish the exact crafting cost.

The calculator therefore defaults to 800 but lets you overwrite the cost with the value shown in Server 504's current Chip Factory UI.
