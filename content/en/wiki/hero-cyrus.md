# Cyrus

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Rider DEF / faction-wide Rider damage support

Cyrus is a Rider combat hero focused on **defensive faction support** with a high-star faction-wide damage contribution.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Cyrus from:

- Elite Recruitment Tickets in the Radio Station;
- the Goodie Bazaar event.

## Skill structure

### Sky-Shattering Strike — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill using the common Orange-hero pattern.

### Axe Strike — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Training Expert: Rider — Passive Skill

Public data describes Cyrus as increasing Rider DEF while star progression adds Rider ATK. At the highest listed breakpoint, he also grants **Rider battle damage** whether deployed or not.

That last effect matters when evaluating replacement cost: a hero can leave the main march and still contribute to the faction account-wide.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Rider roster support / medium replacement cost.**

Cyrus is especially useful when:

- Rider is the account's main faction;
- his star level has reached the faction-wide passive breakpoint;
- a secondary Rider formation needs a durable core;
- replacing him would remove permanent faction damage that remains active off-march.

For a low-star Cyrus, newer combat heroes may offer better direct return. For a highly developed Cyrus, his account-wide contribution changes that calculation.

## Exclusive Equipment

As an Orange hero, Cyrus is eligible for the game's Exclusive Equipment system once the relevant State and star requirements are met. Current hero-specific effects need in-game verification.
