# Pet Roster & Unlock Path

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Official for Regulus condition; community-backed for earlier Pet roster and unlock sequence.

Pet Agents follow a staged progression model. Public community guides describe early and mid-game Agents that unlock as the previous Agent reaches specific level milestones.

## Community-documented early roster

### Sentinel — Green / C-tier

Community role: resource gathering and logistics.

Reported active skill: **Instant Loot**, which instantly clears/collects a resource point.

A current 2026 community guide reports Sentinel Lv.30 as the milestone that unlocks **Shining**.

### Shining — Blue / B-tier

Community role: troop expansion / army support.

Reported active skill: **Rapid Mod**, granting a batch of the account's current highest-tier troops.

### Zeus — Blue / B-tier

Community role: Pet-resource economy.

Reported active skill: **Wilderness Gift**, generating Tactical Rations and other Pet-growth materials.

## Later Agents

Current veteran-community guidance also references **Titan, Frost and Specter** as important later Pet Agents, with Titan commonly treated as the first priority among that group.

The public sources available to this Wiki do not yet provide a clean, internally consistent unlock table for all three. Their exact rarity, required previous-Agent level and rescue requirements therefore remain `VERIFY SERVER 504`.

## Regulus — S-tier

Regulus is the cleanest late-game unlock because the condition is official.

**Official requirement:**

- Titan reaches **Lv.50**;
- complete Regulus rescue tasks.

Exclusive skill: **King's Decree**, temporarily increasing Troop ATK.

See [Regulus](#/wiki/pet-regulus) for the dedicated profile.

## Do not over-read community tier labels

C/B/A/S labels in community Pet guides are useful orientation, but they are not a substitute for the current in-game rarity label. Server 504 screenshots should eventually become the canonical roster source.
