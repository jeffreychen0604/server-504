## Progression specialists

### Evans

Evans is a Shooter-faction Vehicle Transformation specialist. His progression skill supplies Gears and later improves Modified Vehicle economy, movement speed and skill damage. This gives him value to APC progression even if he is not the preferred combat slot in a mature Shooter formation.

### Megan

Megan is a Shooter-faction Construction specialist. Her third skill increases free construction time and construction speed as stars increase. Construction specialists should be evaluated partly by account-economy value rather than formation damage alone.

## Combat specialists

### Natasha

Natasha is a Shooter / Combat hero with Shooter DEF and ATK support. At the highest listed star breakpoint her passive also contributes Shooter battle damage whether deployed or not.

The public source contains a copy-text error naming Cyrus inside Natasha's passive description; the faction field and stat lines still consistently indicate Shooter.

### Margaret

Margaret is a Shooter / Combat hero with a durability-oriented passive that increases Shooter HP and then adds Shooter battle damage through star progression.

### Darian

Darian is a Shooter / Combat hero with an offense-oriented kit. Public data describes a Shooter attack passive and additional troop skill damage through its star progression. Official patch history also confirms Darian has Exclusive Equipment and that its activation animation has received later optimization.

### Rosa

Rosa is a later Shooter / Combat hero. Her public passive combines Shooter damage with reduced counterattack damage taken by the troop she leads, giving her a different defensive/offensive profile from Margaret or Darian.
