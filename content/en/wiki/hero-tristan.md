# Tristan

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Fighter  
> **Specialty:** Fighter DEF / faction-wide Fighter damage support

Tristan is a Fighter combat hero built around **defensive faction support** rather than a unique account-economy mechanic.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Tristan from:

- Elite Recruitment Tickets in the Radio Station;
- the Goodie Bazaar event.

## Skill structure

### Rain Fire — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill using the common Orange-hero scaling pattern.

### Rapid Fire — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Training Expert: Assaulter — Passive Skill

Public data describes Tristan as increasing Fighter DEF and then adding Fighter ATK through star progression. The highest listed breakpoint also grants a faction-wide **Fighter battle damage** increase whether Tristan is deployed or not.

This makes Tristan more valuable than a simple early-game stat stick because part of his end-state value can persist outside the active formation.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Fighter roster support / medium replacement cost.**

Tristan fits best when:

- Fighter is the account's primary faction;
- the account needs more frontline durability;
- his high-star faction-wide passive is already unlocked;
- a secondary Fighter formation still needs a stable combat hero.

He should not automatically receive the same investment priority as a newer Fighter hero with a stronger unique kit. The key question is whether his faction-wide passive has already become part of the account's permanent value.

## Exclusive Equipment

As an Orange hero, Tristan is eligible for the game's Exclusive Equipment system once the relevant State and hero-star requirements are met. Current hero-specific equipment effects require in-game verification.
