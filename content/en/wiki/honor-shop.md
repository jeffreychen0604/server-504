# Honor Shop

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for the official merger/conversion rules; medium for exact current Server 504 stock.  
> **Server 504 status:** Verify the live UI because rollout was staged by State.

The Honor Shop is the unified exchange system introduced to replace several older PvP/event shops.

## Official migration

Official update notes state that these shops are merged into the **Honor Shop**:

- Capital Shop;
- Black Gold Shop;
- Arena Shop.

The same update replaces currencies earned from:

- State of Supremacy;
- Capital Clash;
- Black Gold Battlefield

with **Honor Points**.

### Currency conversion

The official conversion announced was:

- **1 Capital Glory Badge = 40 Honor Points**;
- **1 Black Gold Coin = 2 Honor Points**.

## Why older guides look different

Many 2025 item pages still list Power Cores, Boost Ores, Orange Versatile Hero Fragments, Titanium Alloy or Design Blueprints under Capital/Arena/Black Gold shops.

Those pages are useful for historical source mapping, but the shop name can now be obsolete after the Honor migration.

## Current high-value categories

Recent 2026 community guides report the Honor Shop as a source for high-end equipment progression such as:

- Orange equipment / choice boxes;
- Power Cores;
- DX Blueprints.

Exact inventory, cost and weekly limits must be checked in Server 504.

## Suggested priority logic

For an account still building the main formation:

1. guaranteed Orange equipment or targeted equipment progression;
2. Power Cores once Orange gear reaches the relevant upgrade stage;
3. DX Blueprints when red-equipment breakthrough is actually reachable;
4. secondary/farmable materials only after bottlenecks above are covered.

This is a decision framework, not a fixed price table.
