# Shelter Buildings — Overview

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Official foundation + community building map  
> **Scope:** Permanent shelter systems, not season buildings

Dark War: Survival is built around a shelter-management layer as well as world-map combat. The official store description explicitly describes assigning survivors to jobs, monitoring **health and happiness**, constructing/expanding the shelter, gathering resources, researching and training.

## Building families

For practical planning, shelter structures can be grouped into six systems:

1. **Survivor support** — Dorm, Kitchen, Infirmary, Deerhunter Bar and survivor job stations.
2. **Resource production** — Lumber Mill, Scrapyard, Power Plant and Hunter's Hut.
3. **Protection & recovery** — Warehouse, Hospital, Infirmary, City Gate and Conscription Office.
4. **Development speed** — Library, Construction Hall, Research Center, Alliance Hall and Training Ground.
5. **Military capacity** — Military Center plus Fighter / Rider / Shooter Camps.
6. **Utility & progression** — Radar, Radio Station, Mart, Alliance Plaza, Adventure Camp and other unlocks.

## Why this layer matters

A high Watchtower does not automatically mean a healthy account. Shelter throughput determines whether the account can actually support long research timers, large troop queues, recovery after war and sustained resource spending.

## Unlock-table warning

Community structure lists are useful for orientation, but several public pages conflict on exact unlock levels. Example: one structure index places **Dorm at Watchtower 3**, while a separate Dorm page lists it as available earlier. For that reason, exact unlock levels are treated as **versioned community data** until confirmed from current Server 504 UI.
