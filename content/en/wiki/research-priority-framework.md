# Research Priority Framework

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Purpose:** Decide what to research next without relying on a timeless tier list

There is no single permanent research order that works for every Dark War: Survival account. The best next technology depends on account age, main faction, event schedule, current bottleneck and available Wisdom Medals.

This framework is designed to remain useful even when individual node values change.

## Step 1 — Remove hard progression gates

Research first when it unlocks or unblocks something structural:

- Formation/march availability;
- Watchtower or building prerequisite;
- troop-tier progression;
- required branch prerequisite;
- major Duel milestone reward access;
- critical Battle Strategy breakpoint before war.

A hard gate normally beats a small percentage optimization elsewhere.

## Step 2 — Protect compounding efficiency

After hard gates, prioritize effects used every day:

- Research Speed;
- Construction/Training efficiency;
- formation capacity or march utility;
- gathering throughput;
- hospital/recovery throughput.

Small permanent efficiency gains compound over months.

## Step 3 — Build the Medal engine

If Duel Research is incomplete, evaluate whether the next Duel node increases recurring point/reward efficiency enough to justify its Wisdom Medal cost.

Nodes such as Incentive Gathering, Incentive Zombies, Victory Tycoon, Richer Rewards and One More are widely prioritized because they can improve future Alliance Duel output.

## Step 4 — Strengthen the main war formation

Once event-economy requirements are under control, prioritize Battle/Battle Strategy effects that benefit the faction and formation actually deployed.

Prefer:

- universal troop capacity/broad bonuses;
- main-faction ATK/DEF/HP;
- high-value combat mechanics;
- prerequisites leading to those nodes.

Avoid spreading scarce resources evenly only for cosmetic CP growth.

## Step 5 — Time non-urgent research

If a node can safely wait, complete it during a window that rewards one or more of:

- Tech CP;
- Research Speedups;
- Wisdom Medal consumption.

This produces event value without changing the underlying research cost.

## Decision matrix

| Situation | Likely priority |
| --- | --- |
| Next WT/build is blocked | prerequisite research |
| Duel rewards still inefficient | Duel Research |
| Major war approaching | Battle / Battle Strategy breakpoint |
| Research queues are slow | Development / Research Speed |
| Main formation lacks capacity | Troop Expansion / Formation-related progression |
| Ordinary resources are chronic bottleneck | Economy |
| Industrial construction consumes all speedups | delay non-critical long research |
| No urgent gate and matching event is near | stage research for scoring overlap |

## Confidence rule

A strategy recommendation is not the same as a verified mechanic.

The Wiki will distinguish:

- **Official:** confirmed by patch/current game text.
- **Community-backed:** repeated current observations or guides.
- **Server 504 verified:** current in-game screenshot/evidence.
- **Pending:** useful claim that still needs capture.

## What not to optimize for

Do not choose research mainly because it gives:

- the largest displayed CP number;
- the shortest timer;
- the cheapest resource cost;
- a recommendation copied from a different account stage.

The correct question is: **what permanent constraint does this research remove or what recurring value does it create?**
