> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** S-rarity / Orange heroes with enough public evidence for a stable first-pass profile.  
> **Source policy:** Faction, specialty and acquisition are stored separately from meta rankings. Conflicts remain visible until Server 504 in-game evidence resolves them.

The Hero Database is designed as a **reference layer**, not a permanent tier list. A hero can remain correctly classified even when the combat meta changes because of new heroes, Exclusive Equipment, Industrial progression or balance patches.

## Conflict queue

### Lan Yan

Lan Yan is **not yet placed into the canonical faction table**. One current DarkWarData page describes her in the prose as a Fighter and gives a Fighter-oriented passive, while the structured faction row on the same page says Shooter. Until the Server 504 hero index or a clean independent current source resolves this, the Wiki should not silently pick one.

### Katrina / Katerina spelling

Community databases consistently use **Katrina**, while an official 2025 patch note refers to **Katerina** when mentioning an Exclusive Equipment animation optimization alongside Darian. This may be a localization or patch-note spelling difference. Server 504's current English UI should decide the canonical spelling.

### Guy specialty

DarkWarData describes Guy as an Adventure-type hero in its introduction while its structured `Type` row labels him Combat. Both his Adventure food/energy passive and combat use are real, so the database leaves the type unresolved rather than discarding one side of the evidence.

## Why specialties matter

Not every S-rarity hero exists only to increase battlefield damage. Several provide long-term account utility:

- **Quinn** — unit training / Alliance Duel progression value;
- **Corleone** — research economy and speed;
- **Megan** — construction economy and speed;
- **Evans** — Modified Vehicle / Gear progression;
- **Catherine & Rex** — Radar progression;
- **Guy** — Adventure food and energy utility.

This means a hero can be valuable even when they are not part of the strongest current PvP formation.

## Exclusive Equipment rule

Official patch notes state that the Hero Exclusive Weapon / Exclusive Equipment system:

- unlocks after the State has progressed for a required period;
- applies to **Orange-quality heroes**;
- opens the hero's Exclusive Equipment slot at **2 Stars**;
- adds troop damage, troop HP and stronger faction-counter effects;
- unlocks an additional special combat skill.

Later official updates added Red Star breakthrough, Hall of Honor progression and star-level swapping between Red Star Exclusive Equipment using a **Core of Rotation**.

Because Exclusive Equipment can materially change a hero's value, the Wiki must keep **hero identity data** separate from **current investment ranking**.

## Data fields for future individual hero pages

Each hero page should eventually store:

1. canonical name;
2. rarity;
3. faction;
4. combat role / progression specialty;
5. acquisition sources;
6. Active Skill;
7. Normal Attack;
8. passive or progression skill;
9. Exclusive Talent;
10. Exclusive Equipment name and effects, when verified;
11. season / release context;
12. current usefulness notes with a separate meta verification date;
13. source links and Server 504 screenshots.
