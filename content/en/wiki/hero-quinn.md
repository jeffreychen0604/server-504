# Quinn

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Rider  
> **Specialty:** Alliance Duel / unit training progression

Quinn is a Rider hero whose long-term value comes from **account progression and Alliance Duel scoring**, not only direct combat.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Quinn as recruitable through the **Radio Station**.

## Skill structure

### Devastator's Blade — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the standard S-rarity skill pattern.

### Swordsmanship — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Butcher Instructor — Progression Skill

This is the defining part of Quinn's profile. Public data lists:

- reduced basic resource cost for unit training;
- increasing unit training speed as stars rise;
- increasing **Alliance Duel points** as stars rise;
- at the highest listed star breakpoint, a very large training-speed increase and **+50% Alliance Duel points**.

Exact current values should still be checked in Server 504 before using them in a scoring calculator.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Progression utility / keep invested.**

Quinn is a strong example of why the Wiki does not rank every hero only by PvP damage. Even if a different Rider replaces her in a main combat formation, her unit-training and Alliance Duel value can justify continued investment.

Best-fit use cases:

- Alliance Duel preparation;
- unit-training days;
- accounts trying to increase score efficiency per saved training resource;
- secondary Rider formation when combat stats are sufficient.

## Exclusive Equipment

Quinn is Orange quality and therefore belongs to the hero quality eligible for the game's Exclusive Equipment system once the State and hero-star requirements are met. Her hero-specific Exclusive Equipment details still require current in-game capture before the Wiki publishes a definitive effect table.
