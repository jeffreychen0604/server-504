# Capital & Turrets

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for the current rally/reinforcement rules because they are confirmed in official patch notes.

Capital and Turrets are major state-level combat objectives used in Capital Clash and State of Supremacy contexts.

## Current rally and reinforcement model

Official December 2025 patch notes changed the old single-rally/single-reinforcement behavior:

- rally limits for **Capital** and **Turrets** were removed;
- survivors can launch **multiple rallies** to the same objective;
- survivors can send **multiple reinforcement squads**;
- when launching a rally or reinforcement to a Capital/Turret, troop capacity receives an additional **+40,000**.

This is a current mechanic and should override older guides that describe one rally queue per objective.

## Strategic consequence

The objective is no longer simply “fill one rally and one garrison.” Modern fights can involve parallel rally cycles and multiple reinforcement streams. This increases the importance of:

- rally-lead selection;
- reinforcement timing;
- formation/faction coordination;
- hospital capacity and healing planning;
- keeping secondary marches available instead of committing every march at once.

## Capital Clash schedule visibility

Official February 2026 notes added a phase schedule directly to the Capital Clash event page, helping alliances coordinate preparation and battle windows from the game UI.

## State Hospital connection

For State of Supremacy, official notes warn that untreated units remaining in the State Hospital are permanently lost when the hospital closes at **Tuesday 00:00 server time** after the event.

## Presidency and governance

Capturing or controlling the Capital can connect to state presidency and official-position systems, but the game mechanic does not prescribe a Server 504 political rotation. Any rotation or shared-governance arrangement is a separate server policy.
