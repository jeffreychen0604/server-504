# Watchtower Progression Reference

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for the overall progression model; medium for exact prerequisite rows because current community references disagree on several levels.

The Watchtower is the main progression gate for shelter growth. It raises the ceiling for buildings, research access and troop development, but the cost shown on the Watchtower is only part of the real upgrade cost: prerequisite buildings must also be completed first.

## High-confidence progression pattern

Multiple Dark War: Survival references agree on the following broad structure:

- early levels use basic shelter buildings as prerequisites;
- **Alliance Hall** becomes the recurring bottleneck from roughly WT9 onward;
- **Research Center** reappears at major milestones;
- Fighter, Rider and Shooter Camps rotate through the prerequisite chain at higher levels;
- Iron and Electricity become part of the resource burden around the mid-game;
- WT30 is the transition point into Industrial progression.

## Cost data warning

Different public tables mix two different kinds of values:

- **base / unmodified cost**;
- cost after construction-resource reduction from heroes such as Megan.

For example, late-WT tables published by community sources often show values already reduced by Megan's construction skill. Do not compare those directly with a base-cost table.

The Server 504 Wiki should only publish a definitive WT1–WT30 cost calculator after capturing the current in-game cost display and recording whether construction buffs are active.

## Practical progression rule

When a Watchtower upgrade starts, immediately prepare the prerequisite for the next Watchtower level in the second construction queue whenever possible. This reduces idle progression time and prevents Alliance Hall or a faction camp from becoming the next hard stop.
