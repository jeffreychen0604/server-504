# Chip Factory Recipes Reference

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for material type and official material sources; medium for exact Orange crafting cost; Purple cost still needs Server 504 capture.

Chip Factory is the targeted-crafting path for vehicle chips. It is strategically different from random Chip Chests because it lets you work toward a specific faction/type combination.

## Stable structure

Community references consistently describe:

- **Purple Chips → Common Chip Materials**
- **Orange Chips → Advanced Chip Materials**

Official patch history confirms the Chip System and that Chip Materials were added to Daily Alliance Duel point chests at tiers **3, 6 and 9**. Zombie Siege also received Random Blue Chip Chests in the same rollout.

## Chip Chest material observations

A widely used community guide reports:

- Purple Chip Chest: 30 Advanced Chip Materials plus a chip roll;
- Orange Chip Chest: 100 Advanced Chip Materials plus a guaranteed Orange chip.

Treat those chest yields as **community data**, not official constants, until Server 504 screenshots confirm them.

## Important current rules

Official version history confirms:

- Chip Materials come from Daily Alliance Duel point chests 3 / 6 / 9;
- Zombie Siege includes Random Blue Chip Chests;
- later packs were redesigned to include Mod Vehicle Material Chests and Random Purple/Green Chip Chests;
- Tactical Modification can unlock if any Orange vehicle chip reaches **10★**.
