> **Server 504 naming:** Season 4 · Sealed Island  
> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for Server 504 season naming and officially patched Sealed Island systems; medium for community-only side-event details.

## Core progression loop

A useful simplified model is:

1. obtain seasonal building resources;
2. develop **Quartz Factories**;
3. use Quartz/season resources to upgrade the **Oni Seal Hall**;
4. raise **Virus Resistance**;
5. defeat stronger infected enemies and participate in higher-resistance objectives;
6. manage **Miasma** generated by seasonal combat;
7. participate in Blood Moon and Oni King escalation phases.

For most players, seasonal resistance progression matters more than simply adding more raw formation CP when a resistance wall is blocking content.

## Seasonal resources

Community references consistently identify:

- **Stone** — a seasonal building resource;
- **Quartz** — produced through Quartz Factories and used for advanced seasonal progression;
- event currencies/items such as **Magatama** or **Omamori** during specific activities.

Exact costs, production rates and shop values are intentionally not frozen in this overview because public tables are version-sensitive.

## Blood Moon and seasonal enemies

Official patch history confirms the following Sealed Island entities/systems:

- **Rotting Oni**;
- **Booze Brute**;
- **Blood Moon Rotting Oni**;
- **Blood Moon Booze Brute**;
- **Oni King**;
- **Blood Moon Shroud**;
- **Miasma Bursts Alert**.

The developers changed recommended CP, Viral Resistance and Oni King HP after launch. That makes dated verification essential for any numeric guide.

## Official balance changes worth remembering

In the November 2025 update, the developers:

- lowered recommended CP for Lv.1–65 Rotting Onis and Booze Brutes;
- reduced Viral Resistance requirements for Lv.65+ Blood Moon variants;
- increased Oni King HP because it was dying before enough survivors could participate.

A later update also changed the Sealed Island interface during **Miasma Bursts Alert**, replacing the normal Miasma icon with an **Oni King Statue** icon.

Some public guides call Sealed Island “Season 5.” Server 504 uses **Season 4 — Sealed Island**, and the Server 504 Wiki follows the numbering visible in our server context rather than overwriting it with a third-party sequence.
