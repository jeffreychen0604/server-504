# State of Supremacy

> **Last verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Source confidence:** High for current official cross-state, Capital/Turret and State Hospital rules; medium for older preparation-phase scoring tables.

**State of Supremacy** is a cross-state competition that combines preparation scoring with large-scale PvP and Capital control.

## High-level phases

Community event references describe three broad phases:

1. **Preparation** — multi-day progression scoring similar to Alliance Duel.
2. **War** — cross-state PvP and Capital/Turret fighting.
3. **Recovery / State Hospital settlement** — treatment and revival-related mechanics after the main battle.

The exact event interface should be checked each cycle because the developers continue to adjust cross-state rules.

## Cross-state movement

Official March 2026 changes improved cross-state teleporting:

- during supported cross-state events, including State of Supremacy, players can select a relocation position directly on the opponent State map;
- Advanced Teleporters can be used for relocation in the Lost Lands Capital area;
- same-State survivors are visually differentiated during cross-state activity.

Official notes also state that during State of Supremacy, players cannot attack troops belonging to other survivors from their own State. This reduces accidental friendly fire.

## Capital and Turret combat

Official December 2025 changes removed the old single-rally/single-reinforcement limits for the Capital and Turrets.

Players can now:

- launch multiple rallies against these objectives;
- send multiple reinforcement squads;
- receive an additional **40,000 troop capacity** when launching a rally or reinforcing a Capital/Turret target.

Because this materially changes command structure, older guides describing a one-target-at-a-time restriction are outdated.

## Winning the Capital fight

Older community references describe **Capital occupation time** as a major victory condition. For practical coordination, leadership should treat the event as an objective-control battle rather than a simple kill-point contest.

Key operational questions are:

- which alliance or rally leads hold the Capital;
- which teams pressure Turrets;
- who reinforces versus who rallies;
- how hospitals and recovery capacity are managed;
- when to rotate damaged formations;
- how to avoid unnecessary shelter-burning losses outside the main objectives.

## State Hospital

Official December 2025 patch notes warn that after State of Supremacy ends, untreated units in the **State Hospital** must be handled before the hospital closes at **server time Tuesday 00:00**. Units left untreated after closure can be permanently lost.

This deadline is important enough to include in the Wiki, but players should still confirm the current event UI in case the developer changes the schedule.

## Experimental loss-reduction rule

A December 2025 update introduced an **Experimental Feature** on days when State of Supremacy, Alliance Duel Enemy Buster and Capital Clash begin. During the beta, unit losses from attacking shelters were reduced by 50%.

Because the feature was explicitly described as experimental and initially rolled out gradually, it should not be assumed active on every State without checking the current buff/event notice.

## Preparation checklist

Before War Phase, prepare:

- Advanced Teleporters;
- healing speedups/resources and hospital capacity;
- multiple combat-ready formations;
- rally/reinforcement presets;
- shields for noncombatants;
- clear State-level target and Capital/Turret assignments;
- communication channels for rapid rally changes.

## Wiki vs. server operations

This article documents the game mechanics. Server 504-specific diplomacy, NAP decisions, command roles, Capital allocation or cross-alliance military orders belong in the Operational Codex or live Council notice.
