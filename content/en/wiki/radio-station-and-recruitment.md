# Radio Station & Recruitment

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Scope:** Radio Station role, free recruitment and recruitment pools  
> **Confidence:** HIGH for daily free recruit; COMMUNITY for exact ticket pools and building-level cooldown values

The **Radio Station** is the shelter's hero-recruitment facility. It is where Recruitment Tickets and higher-tier recruitment tickets are converted into hero pulls.

## Current high-confidence behavior

Apple's official Dark War: Survival editorial explicitly identifies a **free daily recruit** at the Radio Station. This makes the Radio Station part of the daily account-maintenance loop even when you are saving paid/earned tickets for a larger recruitment session.

## Recruitment types

Public community data documents multiple recruitment-ticket classes, including:

- standard **Recruitment Tickets**;
- **Elite Recruitment Tickets**;
- higher-tier/special recruitment tickets such as Prime recruitment in some current hero acquisition descriptions.

The exact hero pool behind each recruitment type is patch- and account-era-sensitive. The Wiki therefore does **not** maintain a permanent hero list here unless the current Radio Station UI or a current official source confirms it.

## Building progression

Older community building tables indicate that upgrading the Radio Station reduces the free-recruit cooldown. Those tables are incomplete and may contain bonus-modified values, so current cooldown reduction per level is kept in the verification queue rather than copied as canonical.

## Practical use

- Claim the free recruit before daily rollover.
- Treat ticket type as an acquisition gate, not simply a higher rarity label.
- Before spending rare tickets, check whether the current hero pool contains the hero you actually need.
- Save recruitment resources when a current event explicitly scores recruitment/ticket consumption; do not assume every recruitment event uses the same scoring table.
