# Alliance Centers, Territory & Strongholds

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** High for official 2026 season-specific changes; universal territory rules remain versioned because conquest mechanics differ by season.

Alliance structures and territory systems become increasingly important in later seasonal conquest. The key caution is that **territory rules are not static across every season**.

## Alliance Centers

Alliance Centers are attackable alliance structures used in territory and war systems. Official February 2026 patch notes explicitly reference alliance centers as structures whose durability can be demolished with repeated attacks.

Community Glory War documentation describes an ordered attack path for enemy Alliance Centers:

**Alliance Center I → Alliance Center II → Alliance Center III**

and reports that Alliance Centers can contribute event-specific Glory Point bonuses. Because those values belong to Glory War, they should not be generalized to every season.

## Territory / tiles

Territory connects alliance or camp-controlled structures. Current official conquest patches show that tile rules can include:

- protected and attackable windows;
- connectable targets such as allied tiles, camp cities and oases;
- garrisoning;
- reinforcement caps;
- short post-capture protection/truce periods;
- durability and on-fire mechanics.

These values have been actively rebalanced, so the Wiki should always attach a season/version label to exact tile numbers.

## Strongholds

Strongholds are major territorial control points in conquest seasons.

Official April 2026 changes include:

- Strongholds and tiles inside their area can be protected outside designated War Declaration periods;
- capturing an enemy-camp Stronghold or a neutral Stronghold inside enemy territory can trigger a **1-hour truce**;
- Origin Lands Strongholds were repositioned;
- after capture, members of the owner's camp can teleport shelters inside the Stronghold region;
- enemy-camp members cannot teleport into that controlled Stronghold region;
- Stronghold protection was standardized to **60 minutes** in the cited Origin Lands update.

These are **Origin Lands / conquest-season rules**, not permanent global rules.

## City capture and truce

The same April 2026 official notes adjusted Territory Triumph:

- War Declaration duration was reduced from 4 hours to **2.5 hours**;
- capturing a Neutral / Enemy Camp city triggers a **1.5-hour truce**;
- other captures use a much shorter truce window.

Again, this is versioned conquest content.

## Cross-state protection example

Mutant Mines documentation also shows that territory/Watchtower attack permissions can change by zone: cross-state players may be prevented from attacking Watchtowers outside Lost Lands while those protections are removed in the Capital area.

This is why the Wiki stores `season`, `event`, `zone` and `version` alongside any territory rule.
