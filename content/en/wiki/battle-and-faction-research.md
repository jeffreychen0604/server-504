# Battle & Faction Research

> **Last verified:** 2026-08-08  
> **Confidence:** Community-backed branch structure; exact node values pending current UI capture

Battle research converts account progression into direct troop performance. Unlike Development and Economy, these technologies are usually judged by how much they improve the formation you actually field.

## Core combat dimensions

Public community references consistently describe Battle research around improvements such as:

- troop ATK;
- troop DEF;
- troop HP;
- troop capacity / expansion;
- Fighter-specific bonuses;
- Rider-specific bonuses;
- Shooter-specific bonuses;
- other combat modifiers unlocked deeper in the tree.

The exact current node order and level caps are not yet published as canonical on this Wiki.

## Main-faction logic

Research should generally reinforce the faction and formation you use most often rather than spreading equally across every troop type from the beginning.

A practical sequence is:

1. universal troop-capacity or broad combat improvements;
2. the primary faction used by your strongest formation;
3. secondary formation support;
4. off-faction completion later when the cost is justified.

This does **not** mean faction research should ignore the counter system. Fighter, Rider and Shooter still interact through faction counters, so broad account strength eventually matters. The priority question is simply where the next scarce research resource creates the most immediate value.

## Troop Expansion

Current 2026 community strategy guides repeatedly identify **Troop Expansion** as a high-value early Battle target because formation capacity improves the number of units benefiting from every other stat bonus.

This is a strategy recommendation, not an official universal "best node" rule. Its value depends on current capacity, hero formation, troop tier and research cost.

## Research CP is not combat efficiency

Two technologies with similar CP gain can have very different practical effects. Evaluate Battle research using:

- actual main-squad stat affected;
- whether the effect applies to all troops or one faction;
- prerequisite cost;
- research time;
- whether it unlocks a deeper key node;
- whether the research can be timed with a scoring event.

## Relationship to Battle Strategy

Do not confuse the ordinary **Battle** tree with **Battle Strategy**.

Battle Strategy is a separate advanced Wisdom Medal-gated system used for competitive combat/event progression. Official 2026 patch history explicitly references `Research Center → Battle Strategy → Kill Training`.

See [Battle Strategy & Kill Training](#/wiki/battle-strategy-and-kill-training).
