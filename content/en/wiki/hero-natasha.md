# Natasha

> **Identity verified:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Meta reviewed:** 7 Aug 2026  
> **Rarity:** S / Orange  
> **Faction:** Shooter  
> **Specialty:** Shooter DEF / faction-wide Shooter damage support

Natasha is a Shooter combat hero focused on **defensive faction support** with an account-wide Shooter damage payoff at high star progression.

## Acquisition

Community data lists Natasha from **Elite Recruitment Tickets** in the Radio Station.

## Skill structure

### Crushing Kiss — Active Skill

Direct-damage Active Skill following the common Orange-hero scaling pattern.

### Phantom — Normal Attack

Standard Normal Attack progression.

### Training Expert: Marksman — Passive Skill

Public data describes Natasha as increasing Shooter DEF while star progression adds Shooter ATK. At the highest listed breakpoint, the skill also grants **Shooter battle damage** whether Natasha is deployed or not.

The community page contains an obvious copy-text error that names Cyrus inside Natasha's passive description. The faction field and the passive's actual stat lines consistently point to **Shooter**, so the Wiki preserves the mechanic but records the source-quality issue.

### High Morale — Exclusive Talent

Public data lists hero ATK +10%, DEF +10% and troop capacity +5%.

## Current usefulness

**Decision label: Shooter roster support / medium replacement cost.**

Natasha is especially useful when:

- Shooter is the main faction;
- her high-star faction-wide passive is already unlocked;
- a secondary Shooter formation needs more durability;
- replacing her would remove permanent Shooter damage that stays active off-march.

For a low-star Natasha, compare her direct contribution against newer Shooter combat heroes before investing heavily.
