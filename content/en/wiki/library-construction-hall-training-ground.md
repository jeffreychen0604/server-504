# Library, Construction Hall & Training Ground

> **Last verified:** 8 Aug 2026  
> **Confidence:** Library/Construction Hall community-backed; Training Ground exact effect requires current UI verification

These buildings are best understood as **throughput multipliers** rather than direct resource generators.

## Library

Community building references describe the Library as increasing **Research Speed**. An archived effect table scales the bonus by building level, but the exact percentages remain version-sensitive and are not treated as canonical here.

## Construction Hall

Recent 2026 community guides describe the Construction Hall as reducing construction time / increasing construction throughput. Because construction timers become extremely long later, even modest permanent speed can compound across months of upgrades.

## Training Ground

Public references are much weaker for the Training Ground. Community strategy guides say it contributes only a relatively small troop-training speed increase compared with the `Efficient Training` research node, but the current building tooltip/effect table is not cleanly documented.

Therefore:

- **Library function:** research-speed support — high confidence community.
- **Construction Hall function:** construction-speed support — current community consensus.
- **Training Ground function/value:** keep provisional until current Server 504 UI is captured.

## Priority logic

Speed buildings have compounding value when upgraded **before** a long sequence of timers. However, they should not block hard prerequisites or scarce-material progression merely for a small percentage gain.

Use this sequence:

`hard prerequisite → permanent throughput multiplier → long-duration upgrade/research`
